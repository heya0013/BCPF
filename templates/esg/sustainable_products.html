可持续产品 Sustainable Products

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

合规与标准 Compliance and Standards
核心价值 Core Value

建立产品安全与合格评定的技术文档与符合性声明口径，确保合规标识与投放市场要求。 Ensure product safety and compliance through technical documentation and conformity statements for market access.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 将内部指标与证据映射到 GRI、ISSB(IFRS S1/S2)、ESRS、CDP 的披露条目，形成模板化产出。 Map internal metrics and evidence to GRI, ISSB (IFRS S1/S2), ESRS, CDP disclosure items to create standardized reporting templates.
Best Practice
产品生命周期管理 Product Life-cycle Mgmt.
核心价值 Core Value

建立可持续产品的指标、方法与证据体系，确保合规性、可追溯与市场准入。 Define sustainability metrics and evidence to ensure compliance and traceability.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 建立 ESG 数据台账（活动数据、排放因子、边界与方法），支持 Scope 1/2/3 自动核算与可追溯。 Create ESG data ledger (activity data, emission factors, boundaries, methods) to enable automated Scope 1/2/3 calculation and traceability.
Best Practice
可持续材料管理
Sustainable Material Mgmt.
核心价值

建立可持续产品的指标、方法与证据体系，确保合规性、可追溯与市场准入。 Define sustainability metrics and evidence to ensure compliance and traceability.

优化建议：
  • 基于供方分层与品类模型建立一次数据收集与模型估算并行机制，逐步提升一次数据占比。
    • Build parallel mechanism for primary data collection and model estimation based on supplier tiers and category models to increase primary data share.
Best Practice
产品生命周期终端管理
Product End of Life Mgmt
核心价值

建立循环性度量（再生成分、可维修/可回收、回收去向）与 EPR/WEEE 合规口径，形成产品端闭环管理。 Set circularity measures (recycled content, repairability, recyclability) and EPR/WEEE compliance for end-of-life management.

优化建议：
  • 开展温室气体减排目标设定与情景分析，量化转型/物理风险并对齐资本规划。 Set GHG reduction targets and perform scenario analysis to align with capital planning.
