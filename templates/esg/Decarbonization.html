脱碳 Decarbonization

减少或消除产品全生命周期的碳排放 Reducing or eliminating carbon emissions throughout product's lifecycle.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

运输二氧化碳排放 Transportation CO₂ emission
核心价值 Core Value

供应链排放应基于 GHG Scope 3 分类开展核算，采用一次数据与模型估算并行并逐步提升一次数据占比 Supply chain emissions shall be accounted for based on GHG Scope 3 classification, adopting the parallel use of primary data and model estimation and gradually increasing the proportion of primary data.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 对接计量 / SCADA / 账单，按 ISO 50001 与 GHG Protocol 组织活动数据与排放因子，统一核算口径 Connect metering/SCADA/bills, organize activity data and emission factors in accordance with ISO 50001 and GHG Protocol, and unify accounting standards.

低碳制造 Low-carbon Manufacturing
核心价值 Core Value

该流程涉及国际通行标准/法规口径，需形成可审计、可追溯与可比的证据体系 Involves internationally accepted standards/regulatory frameworks and requires the establishment of an auditable, traceable and comparable evidence system.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 建立数据血缘、内控点与证据留痕，按 ISAE 3000/IFRS S1/S2 组织有限 / 合理保证材料 Establish data lineage, internal control points and evidence traceability, and organize limited/reasonable assurance materials in accordance with ISAE 3000 and IFRS S1/S2.
  • 按 GRI 303/306 与 ESRS E3/E5 建立取排水 / 废弃物的口径、台账与证据，自动映射披露模板 Establish standards, accounting books and evidence for water intake/discharge and waste in accordance with GRI 303/306 and ESRS E3/E5, and automatically map to disclosure templates.
