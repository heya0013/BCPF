AI应用场景
AI Application Scenarios

票据/记录抽取
Document/Record Extraction

利用自然语言处理和OCR技术自动从ESG相关票据和记录中提取关键数据，包括水资源使用记录、废弃物处理票据和合规文件，确保数据完整性和准确性，提高数据收集效率。 Using natural language processing and OCR technology to automatically extract key data from ESG-related documents and records, including water usage records, waste disposal documents and compliance files, ensuring data integrity and accuracy, improving data collection efficiency.

异常与缺失检测
Anomaly and Missing Detection

通过机器学习算法自动识别ESG数据中的异常值和缺失信息，检测数据不一致性、计算错误和报告偏差，提供实时预警和纠正建议，提高数据质量。 Using machine learning algorithms to automatically identify anomalies and missing information in ESG data, detecting data inconsistencies, calculation errors and reporting deviations, providing real-time alerts and correction suggestions, improving data quality.

披露一致性校验
Disclosure Consistency Verification

基于智能校验技术，自动验证不同框架和标准下ESG披露内容的一致性，识别冲突和矛盾点，确保ESG报告的内外部一致性，提高披露可信度。 Based on intelligent verification technology, automatically verifying the consistency of ESG disclosure content under different frameworks and standards, identifying conflicts and contradictions, ensuring internal and external consistency of ESG reports, improving disclosure credibility.

Real Industry Cases

浩鲸科技"中国ESG大模型（鲸牛ESG）2.0" - AI驱动的跨职能ESG协作平台
Whale Cloud Technology "China ESG Large Model (Whale Bull ESG) 2.0" - AI-driven Cross-functional ESG Collaboration Platform

浩鲸科技联合中国企业改革与发展研究会、责任云研究院发布的"中国ESG大模型（鲸牛ESG）2.0"，是国内首个专注于ESG领域的垂直大模型，标志着我国企业ESG治理迈入"人工智能+"深度赋能的新阶段。该平台通过AI技术实现跨职能协作，显著提升了水资源管理和废弃物处置效率。 Whale Cloud Technology, in collaboration with China Enterprise Reform and Development Society and Responsibility Cloud Research Institute, released the "China ESG Large Model (Whale Bull ESG) 2.0", which is the first vertical large model focusing on the ESG field in China, marking a new stage of "AI+" deep empowerment in corporate ESG governance. The platform achieves cross-functional collaboration through AI technology, significantly improving water resource management and waste disposal efficiency.

通过实施AI驱动的ESG跨职能协作平台，浩鲸科技成功帮助客户将回用率提高了18个百分点，无害化处置率提升了8个百分点，取水强度降低了12%，数据处理准确率达到92%。系统能够精准抽取水资源和废弃物管理数据，智能识别数据异常和缺失，动态校验披露一致性，并提供详细的改进建议和优化策略，大幅提高了ESG管理的效率和可靠性。 By implementing the AI-driven ESG cross-functional collaboration platform, Whale Cloud Technology successfully helped clients increase reuse rate by 18 percentage points, improve harmless disposal rate by 8 percentage points, reduce water intensity by 12%, and achieve data processing accuracy rate of 92%. The system can accurately extract water resource and waste management data, intelligently identify data anomalies and missing values, dynamically verify disclosure consistency, and provide detailed improvement suggestions and optimization strategies, significantly improving the efficiency and reliability of ESG management.

鲸牛ESG大模型2.0特别注重水资源和废弃物管理的跨职能协作，通过AI技术优化资源利用效率和环境绩效。平台整合了数据采集、异常检测和报告校验等多个模块，实现了跨部门的环境管理方法。同时，通过AI驱动的风险评估和预警机制，浩鲸科技大幅提高了问题发现效率，减少了管理偏差，实现了ESG管理的闭环优化。结合GRI 303水与废水管理标准和ESRS E3水资源管理要求，确保水资源管理的准确性和一致性。 The Whale Bull ESG Large Model 2.0 particularly focuses on cross-functional collaboration in water resource and waste management, using AI technology to optimize resource utilization efficiency and environmental performance. The platform integrates multiple modules including data collection, anomaly detection and report verification, achieving a cross-departmental environmental management approach. Meanwhile, through AI-driven risk assessment and early warning mechanisms, Whale Cloud Technology significantly improved problem discovery efficiency, reduced management deviations, achieving closed-loop optimization of ESG management. Combined with GRI 303 Water and Effluents Standard and ESRS E3 Water Resource Management requirements to ensure the accuracy and consistency of water resource management.

该平台已成功应用于多个行业的水资源管理案例，自动识别出生产过程中的水资源消耗热点，并提出循环利用和优化建议。在废弃物管理方面，系统能够追踪废弃物的全生命周期，从产生到处置，确保无害化处理率达标。通过AI技术，企业能够实现水资源和废弃物管理的精细化、智能化，为可持续发展提供有力支持。 The platform has been successfully applied to water resource management cases in multiple industries, automatically identifying water consumption hotspots in production processes and proposing recycling and optimization suggestions. In waste management, the system can track the entire life cycle of waste, from generation to disposal, ensuring harmless treatment rate meets standards. Through AI technology, enterprises can achieve refined and intelligent management of water resources and waste, providing strong support for sustainable development.
