AI应用场景
AI Application Scenarios

证据抽取
Evidence Extraction

利用自然语言处理和机器学习技术自动从ESG报告中抽取关键证据和支持文档，包括审计报告、合规证明和绩效数据，提高证据收集效率和准确性。 Using natural language processing and machine learning technology to automatically extract key evidence and supporting documents from ESG reports, including audit reports, compliance certificates and performance data, improving evidence collection efficiency and accuracy.

控制有效性评估
Control Effectiveness Evaluation

通过智能评估算法自动验证ESG内部控制的有效性，识别控制缺陷和薄弱环节，提供实时评估反馈和改进建议，降低合规风险。 Using intelligent evaluation algorithms to automatically verify the effectiveness of ESG internal controls, identifying control deficiencies and weak links, providing real-time evaluation feedback and improvement suggestions, reducing compliance risk.

异常样本选择
Anomaly Sample Selection

基于机器学习算法智能识别ESG数据中的异常样本和异常模式，优先选择高风险样本进行深入审查，提高审计效率和问题发现能力。 Based on machine learning algorithms, intelligently identifying abnormal samples and abnormal patterns in ESG data, prioritizing high-risk samples for in-depth review, improving audit efficiency and problem discovery capability.

Evidence extraction technology provides automated processing capabilities for ESG evidence, control effectiveness evaluation technology ensures ESG internal control effectiveness, and anomaly sample selection technology identifies high-risk samples and abnormal patterns, providing priority review suggestions and problem discovery strategies, optimizing ESG data transparency and reliability.

AI技术通过分析历史ESG数据、控制模式和审计记录，识别最优控制策略和最佳实践，提供个性化的改进建议和决策支持。控制有效性评估技术考虑了多种动态因素，包括法规变化、行业标准和审计要求，生成最优的控制评估方案和执行策略，最大化控制有效性和合规性。异常样本选择技术基于实时数据和预测模型，构建异常识别体系，对识别出的异常进行量化评估，并实现全流程跟踪与预警，提高审计效率和问题发现能力。结合ISAE 3000鉴证标准和IFRS S1/S2可持续披露标准的最佳实践，确保End-to-end ESG Visibility流程的准确性和一致性。 AI technology analyzes historical ESG data, control patterns and audit records to identify optimal control strategies and best practices, providing personalized improvement suggestions and decision support. Control effectiveness evaluation technology considers multiple dynamic factors, including regulatory changes, industry standards and audit requirements, to generate optimal control evaluation solutions and execution strategies, maximizing control effectiveness and compliance. Anomaly sample selection technology is based on real-time data and prediction models, building anomaly identification systems, quantifying identified anomalies, and achieving full process tracking and early warning, improving audit efficiency and problem discovery capability. Combined with ISAE 3000 Assurance Standards and IFRS S1/S2 Sustainability Disclosure Standards best practices to ensure the accuracy and consistency of End-to-end ESG Visibility processes.

The platform is based on evidence extraction, control effectiveness evaluation and anomaly sample selection technologies, providing comprehensive ESG assurance solutions for global enterprises.

通过实施AI驱动的ESG智能鉴证平台，德勤成功将披露一致性率提高了18个百分点，鉴证发现项数减少了25%，整改关闭周期缩短了28%，ESG数据透明度提升了22%。平台能够精准抽取关键证据信息，智能评估控制有效性，动态选择异常样本，并提供详细的改进建议和优化策略，大幅提高了ESG鉴证的效率和可靠性。 By implementing the AI-driven ESG intelligent assurance platform, Deloitte successfully increased disclosure consistency rate by 18 percentage points, reduced assurance finding items by 25%, shortened rectification closure cycle by 28%, and improved ESG data transparency by 22%. The platform can accurately extract key evidence information, intelligently evaluate control effectiveness, dynamically select abnormal samples, and provide detailed improvement suggestions and optimization strategies, significantly improving the efficiency and reliability of ESG assurance.

德勤的ESG智能鉴证平台特别注重端到端可见性和数据透明度，通过AI技术优化ESG证据收集、控制评估和异常检测。平台整合了证据管理、控制测试和样本选择等多个模块，实现了跨功能的ESG鉴证方法。同时，通过AI驱动的异常检测和风险评估机制，德勤大幅提高了问题发现效率，减少了不必要的审计工作，实现了ESG鉴证的闭环优化。 Deloitte's ESG intelligent assurance platform particularly focuses on end-to-end visibility and data transparency, using AI technology to optimize ESG evidence collection, control evaluation and anomaly detection. The platform integrates multiple modules including evidence management, control testing and sample selection, achieving a cross-functional ESG assurance approach. Meanwhile, through AI-driven anomaly detection and risk assessment mechanisms, Deloitte significantly improved problem discovery efficiency, reduced unnecessary audit work, achieving closed-loop optimization of ESG assurance.
