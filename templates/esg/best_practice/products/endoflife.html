情景模拟
Scenario Simulation

利用AI技术模拟不同产品报废策略的环境影响和经济效益，基于历史数据和市场趋势预测最优报废管理方案，提高决策的科学性和准确性。 Using AI technology to simulate the environmental impact and economic benefits of different product end-of-life strategies, predicting optimal disposal management solutions based on historical data and market trends, improving decision-making science and accuracy.

碳价格/成本敏感性
Carbon Price/Cost Sensitivity

通过AI模型评估不同碳价格情景下产品报废策略的成本效益，分析碳成本对报废决策的影响，优化碳成本管理并提高应对政策变化的能力。 Using AI models to evaluate the cost-effectiveness of product end-of-life strategies under different carbon price scenarios, analyzing the impact of carbon costs on disposal decisions, optimizing carbon cost management and improving the ability to respond to policy changes.

目标路径评估
Target Path Evaluation

基于AI系统持续监控企业碳减排目标与实际进展的差距，提供动态调整建议，确保企业保持在科学碳目标路径上，提高目标达成率。 Based on AI systems continuously monitoring the gap between corporate carbon reduction targets and actual progress, providing dynamic adjustment suggestions, ensuring companies stay on the scientific carbon target path, and improving target achievement rate.

AI技术通过分析历史产品报废数据、碳价格趋势和减排目标进展，识别最优报废管理策略和最佳实践。情景模拟技术考虑了多种动态因素，包括政策变化、市场条件和环境影响，生成最优的产品报废方案和执行策略。碳价格敏感性分析技术评估了不同碳定价机制对报废决策的影响，提供了碳成本优化建议。目标路径评估技术基于实时数据和预测模型，构建目标跟踪体系，对识别出的偏差进行量化评估，并实现全流程跟踪与预警，提高目标管理效率和准确性。 AI technology analyzes historical product end-of-life data, carbon price trends and emission reduction target progress to identify optimal disposal management strategies and best practices. Scenario simulation technology considers multiple dynamic factors, including policy changes, market conditions and environmental impacts, generating optimal product end-of-life solutions and execution strategies. Carbon price sensitivity analysis technology evaluates the impact of different carbon pricing mechanisms on disposal decisions, providing carbon cost optimization suggestions. Target path evaluation technology is based on real-time data and prediction models, building target tracking systems, quantifying identified deviations, and achieving full process tracking and early warning, improving target management efficiency and accuracy.

施耐德电气作为全球能源管理和自动化解决方案的领导者，通过实施AI驱动的产品生命周期末端管理系统，优化了产品报废阶段的碳足迹管理。该系统基于情景模拟、碳价格敏感性分析和目标路径评估技术，为企业提供了全面的产品报废碳管理解决方案。 As a global leader in energy management and automation solutions, Schneider Electric optimized carbon footprint management in the product end-of-life phase by implementing an AI-driven product life-cycle end management system. The system is based on scenario simulation, carbon price sensitivity analysis and target path evaluation technologies, providing comprehensive product end-of-life carbon management solutions for enterprises.

通过实施AI驱动的产品生命周期末端管理系统，施耐德电气成功将SBTi目标批准率提高了12个百分点，目标达成偏差减少了18%，碳成本暴露优化了25%，并减少了超过15,000吨的CO2排放。系统能够精准模拟不同报废策略的碳影响，智能评估碳价格变化对报废决策的影响，动态跟踪碳减排目标进展，并提供实时调整建议，大幅提高了产品报废碳管理的效率和可靠性。 By implementing the AI-driven product end-of-life management system, Schneider Electric successfully increased SBTi target approval rate by 12 percentage points, reduced target achievement deviation by 18%, optimized carbon cost exposure by 25%, and reduced CO2 emissions by more than 15,000 tons. The system can accurately simulate the carbon impact of different disposal strategies, intelligently evaluate the impact of carbon price changes on disposal decisions, dynamically track carbon reduction target progress, and provide real-time adjustment suggestions, significantly improving the efficiency and reliability of product end-of-life carbon management.

施耐德电气的AI驱动平台特别注重产品报废阶段的碳足迹优化和循环经济实践，通过AI技术将报废产品转化为资源，实现闭环材料循环。该公司已将其AI解决方案整合到全球运营中，作为其2025年可持续发展战略的一部分，用于优化大规模产品报废管理，加强其碳管理能力，并推动AI技术在产品生命周期末端管理中的应用。通过与顶级回收合作伙伴合作，施耐德电气能够满足对可持续产品报废管理日益增长的需求。 Schneider Electric's AI-driven platform particularly focuses on carbon footprint optimization and circular economy practices in the product end-of-life phase, using AI technology to transform end-of-life products into resources, achieving closed-loop material cycles. The company has integrated its AI solutions into global operations as part of its 2025 sustainability strategy, to optimize large-scale product end-of-life management, strengthen its carbon management capabilities, and promote the application of AI technology in product life-cycle end management. By partnering with top recycling partners, Schneider Electric is able to meet the growing demand for sustainable product end-of-life management.

施耐德电气的方法已经优化了超过50,000吨的产品报废材料管理，从产品生命周期中去除超过15,000吨CO2当量。除了减少废弃物产生外，施耐德电气还通过将报废产品回收成增值材料，为循环经济提供碳负解决方案。该公司响应市场对可追溯、本地化、低碳产品报废管理日益增长的需求，通过本地化回收和缩短供应链创造优质就业机会并振兴当地经济。 Schneider Electric's approach has optimized the management of over 50,000 tons of product end-of-life materials, removing more than 15,000 tons of CO2 equivalent from the product life cycle. In addition to reducing waste generation, Schneider Electric provides a carbon-negative solution for the circular economy by recycling end-of-life products into value-added materials. The company responds to the increasing market need for traceable, localized, low-carbon product end-of-life management, creating quality jobs and revitalizing local economies through localized recycling and shorter supply chains.
