Transportation CO2 Emission AI案例研究
Transportation CO2 Emission AI Case Study

AI驱动的交通碳排放监测与优化解决方案，通过数据质量监控+异常检测+碳因子匹配+口径一致性校验技术降低能耗强度和碳强度，提升交通系统可持续性 AI-driven transportation carbon emission monitoring and optimization solutions reduce energy intensity and carbon intensity, and enhance transportation system sustainability through data quality monitoring + anomaly detection + carbon factor matching + calibration consistency verification technology

AI应用场景
AI Application Scenarios

数据质量监控
Data Quality Monitoring

利用AI技术自动监测交通能耗和碳排放数据的完整性、准确性和一致性，识别数据异常和缺失值，确保数据质量满足碳排放核算和分析要求。 Using AI technology to automatically monitor the completeness, accuracy and consistency of transportation energy consumption and carbon emission data, identifying data anomalies and missing values, ensuring data quality meets carbon emission accounting and analysis requirements.

异常检测
Anomaly Detection

通过机器学习算法识别交通碳排放数据中的异常模式和异常值，检测设备故障、操作错误和异常排放行为，提供实时预警和异常处理建议。 Using machine learning algorithms to identify abnormal patterns and outliers in transportation carbon emission data, detecting equipment failures, operational errors and abnormal emission behaviors, providing real-time alerts and anomaly handling suggestions.

碳因子匹配
Carbon Factor Matching

基于智能匹配算法，自动识别和匹配最合适的碳排放因子，根据燃料类型、车辆类型和运输模式进行精准计算，提高碳排放核算的准确性和一致性。 Based on intelligent matching algorithms, automatically identifying and matching the most appropriate carbon emission factors, accurately calculating based on fuel type, vehicle type and transportation mode, improving the accuracy and consistency of carbon emission accounting.

口径一致性校验
Calibration Consistency Verification

通过智能校验技术，确保不同部门、不同时期的碳排放数据采用统一的计算标准和方法，避免统计偏差和计算错误，提高数据可比性和可靠性。 Using intelligent verification technology to ensure carbon emission data from different departments and periods adopts unified calculation standards and methods, avoiding statistical deviations and calculation errors, improving data comparability and reliability.

中国科学院空天信息创新研究院研发的"全景AI碳排放走航观测平台"融合全景人工智能与多源走航观测技术，成功实现对城市道路二氧化碳排放量的高时空分辨率精准刻画与溯源。该平台集成全景摄像机、高精度温室气体分析仪、气象要素传感器等多源设备，可同步获取道路场景的三维环境特征、气象参数及二氧化碳排放等信息。 The "Panoramic AI Carbon Emission Mobile Observation Platform" developed by the Chinese Academy of Sciences integrates panoramic artificial intelligence and multi-source mobile observation technology to successfully achieve high spatiotemporal resolution accurate characterization and traceability of urban road carbon dioxide emissions. The platform integrates multiple source equipment such as panoramic cameras, high-precision greenhouse gas analyzers, and meteorological sensors, which can simultaneously obtain 3D environmental features, meteorological parameters and carbon dioxide emission information of road scenes.

通过实施全景AI碳排放走航观测平台，研究团队成功将能耗强度降低了8%，碳强度降低了12%。该平台借助全景AI模型实现二氧化碳排放源的精准提取，平均精度超过93%，平均误差低于1.3ppm。同时，利用全景AI模型中的可解释机器学习模块，研究团队成功量化气象条件、交通流量、道路周边景观三大因素对二氧化碳排放的影响程度。 By implementing the panoramic AI carbon emission mobile observation platform, the research team successfully reduced energy intensity by 8% and carbon intensity by 12%. The platform uses panoramic AI models to achieve accurate extraction of carbon dioxide emission sources, with an average accuracy of over 93% and an average error of less than 1.3ppm. Meanwhile, using the interpretable machine learning module in the panoramic AI model, the research team successfully quantified the influence of three major factors on carbon dioxide emissions: meteorological conditions, traffic flow, and road surrounding landscape.

该技术能够捕捉道路二氧化碳排放量的时空动态变化，刻画城市道路日间逐小时30米空间分辨率的二氧化碳排放动态、来源和驱动因素，清晰识别出不同因素对道路碳排放的影响边界和变化曲线，从而实现城市道路碳排放高分辨率的精准刻画和溯源。目前，该技术已在深圳市投入应用，未来有望推广到其他城市。 This technology can capture the spatiotemporal dynamic changes of road carbon dioxide emissions, characterize the diurnal hourly 30-meter spatial resolution carbon dioxide emission dynamics, sources and driving factors of urban roads, clearly identify the influence boundaries and change curves of different factors on road carbon emissions, thus achieving high-resolution accurate characterization and traceability of urban road carbon emissions. Currently, this technology has been implemented in Shenzhen and is expected to be promoted to other cities in the future.
