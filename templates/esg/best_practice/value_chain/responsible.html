AI应用场景
AI Application Scenarios

证据比对
Evidence Comparison

利用自然语言处理和机器学习技术自动比对供应商提供的环境证据和合规文件，包括可再生能源证书、碳足迹报告和可持续性认证，确保数据一致性和准确性，提高Scope 2计算一致性率。 Using natural language processing and machine learning technology to automatically compare environmental evidence and compliance documents provided by suppliers, including renewable energy certificates, carbon footprint reports and sustainability certifications, ensuring data consistency and accuracy, improving Scope 2 calculation consistency rate.

来源追踪
Source Tracking

通过智能追踪技术自动识别和追踪能源和材料的来源，包括可再生能源类型、地理位置和供应链路径，提高采购透明度和可追溯性，优化可再生能源占比。 Using intelligent tracking technology to automatically identify and track the source of energy and materials, including renewable energy types, geographical locations and supply chain paths, improving procurement transparency and traceability, optimizing renewable energy percentage.

碳因子匹配
Carbon Factor Matching

基于智能匹配算法，自动识别和匹配最合适的碳排放因子，根据能源类型、地域差异和采购模式进行精准计算，提高碳足迹核算的准确性和一致性。 Based on intelligent matching algorithms, automatically identifying and matching the most appropriate carbon emission factors, accurately calculating based on energy type, regional differences and procurement patterns, improving the accuracy and consistency of carbon footprint accounting.

通过AI驱动的Responsible Sourcing优化解决方案，企业能够显著提高Scope 2计算一致性率，提升可再生能源占比，优化采购管理效率和可持续性。证据比对技术提供了对供应商环境证据的自动化验证能力，来源追踪技术则确保了能源和材料来源的透明度和可追溯性，碳因子匹配技术优化了碳足迹核算的准确性和一致性，提高了负责任采购的可靠性和合规性。 Through AI-driven Responsible Sourcing optimization solutions, enterprises can significantly improve Scope 2 calculation consistency rate, enhance renewable energy percentage, optimize procurement management efficiency and sustainability. Evidence comparison technology provides automated verification capabilities for supplier environmental evidence, source tracking technology ensures the transparency and traceability of energy and material sources, and carbon factor matching technology optimizes the accuracy and consistency of carbon footprint accounting, improving the reliability and compliance of responsible sourcing.

AI技术通过分析历史采购数据、能源模式和合规记录，识别最优采购策略和最佳实践，提供个性化的改进建议和决策支持。碳因子匹配技术考虑了多种动态因素，包括能源类型、地域差异和采购模式，生成最优的碳足迹计算方案和执行策略，最大化碳足迹核算的准确性和可靠性。来源追踪技术基于实时数据和区块链技术，构建来源识别体系，对识别出的来源进行量化评估，并实现全流程跟踪与验证，提高采购透明度和可信度。结合GHG Protocol Scope 2指南和RE100倡议的最佳实践，确保Responsible Sourcing流程的准确性和一致性。 AI technology analyzes historical procurement data, energy patterns and compliance records to identify optimal procurement strategies and best practices, providing personalized improvement suggestions and decision support. Carbon factor matching technology considers multiple dynamic factors, including energy type, regional differences and procurement patterns, to generate optimal carbon footprint calculation solutions and execution strategies, maximizing the accuracy and reliability of carbon footprint accounting. Source tracking technology is based on real-time data and blockchain technology, building source identification systems, quantifying identified sources, and achieving full process tracking and verification, improving procurement transparency and credibility. Combined with GHG Protocol Scope 2 Guidance and RE100 Initiative best practices to ensure the accuracy and consistency of Responsible Sourcing processes.

Legendshop朗尊AI采购商城通过将可持续性指标深度植入采购决策全流程，让"不可见"的责任变得"可计算"。平台的核心突破在于构建了责任采购的"神经中枢"，通过AI技术实现了证据比对、来源追踪和碳因子匹配的智能化优化。 Legendshop Langzun AI Procurement Mall deeply embeds sustainability indicators into the entire procurement decision-making process, making "invisible" responsibilities "calculable". The platform's core breakthrough lies in building a "neural center" for responsible procurement, achieving intelligent optimization of evidence comparison, source tracking and carbon factor matching through AI technology.

通过实施AI驱动的Responsible Sourcing解决方案，Legendshop朗尊成功将Scope 2计算一致性率提高了18个百分点，可再生能源占比提升了25个百分点，采购碳排放降低了35%，单店年度碳排放下降了17吨。系统能够精准比对供应商提供的环境证据，智能追踪能源和材料来源，动态匹配最优碳因子，并提供详细的改进建议和优化策略，大幅提高了负责任采购的效率和可靠性。 By implementing the AI-driven Responsible Sourcing solution, Legendshop Langzun successfully increased Scope 2 calculation consistency rate by 18 percentage points, improved renewable energy percentage by 25 percentage points, reduced procurement carbon emissions by 35%, and decreased annual carbon emissions per store by 17 tons. The system can accurately compare environmental evidence provided by suppliers, intelligently track energy and material sources, dynamically match optimal carbon factors, and provide detailed improvement suggestions and optimization strategies, significantly improving the efficiency and reliability of responsible procurement.

Legendshop朗尊的平台特别注重责任采购的价值转化，通过AI技术优化采购流程和碳排放管理。系统整合了供应商ESG智能图谱、碳足迹穿透式追踪和区块链存证等多个模块，实现了跨功能的负责任采购方法。同时，通过AI驱动的风险检测和预警机制，Legendshop朗尊大幅提高了供应链透明度，减少了采购风险，实现了负责任采购的闭环优化。 Legendshop Langzun's platform particularly focuses on the value transformation of responsible procurement, using AI technology to optimize procurement processes and carbon emission management. The system integrates multiple modules including supplier ESG intelligent mapping, carbon footprint penetration tracking and blockchain certification, achieving a cross-functional responsible procurement approach. Meanwhile, through AI-driven risk detection and early warning mechanisms, Legendshop Langzun significantly improved supply chain transparency, reduced procurement risks, achieving closed-loop optimization of responsible procurement.

在国际连锁咖啡品牌的实际应用案例中，Legendshop朗尊平台通过AI技术重构了采购逻辑：在咖啡豆采购中，AI筛选出符合"雨林联盟认证"且运输碳排放最低的种植园；在纸杯供应中，系统自动计算竹纤维杯比传统塑料淋膜杯全生命周期碳排降低62%；在物流优化中，将多个供应商货物AI拼车配送，区域配送里程减少28%。这些优化措施使得单店年度碳排下降17吨，ESG评级跃升带动融资成本降低1.2个百分点。 In the practical application case of an international coffee chain brand, the Legendshop Langzun platform reconstructed the procurement logic through AI technology: in coffee bean procurement, AI screened plantations that met the "Rainforest Alliance Certification" and had the lowest transportation carbon emissions; in paper cup supply, the system automatically calculated that bamboo fiber cups reduced the full life cycle carbon emissions by 62% compared to traditional plastic-coated cups; in logistics optimization, AI consolidated shipments from multiple suppliers, reducing regional delivery mileage by 28%. These optimization measures resulted in a reduction of 17 tons of annual carbon emissions per store, and the ESG rating upgrade led to a reduction in financing costs by 1.2 percentage points.
