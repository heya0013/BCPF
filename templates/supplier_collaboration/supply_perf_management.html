Supply Performance Management
优化供应商协作过程中的协作效率，确保各部门无缝衔接 Optimize collaboration efficiency in the supplier collaboration process, ensuring seamless connectivity between departments.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

供应商绩效跟踪
Supplier Performance Tracking
核心价值 Core Value

PO确认：逐项确认作为排产与ASN依据，影响OTD。；质量协同：NCR/8D迅速闭环，降低PPM与退货。；绩效：用OTD/PPM/响应等指标驱动改进。 PO confirmation: Item-by-item confirmation as the basis for scheduling and ASN, impacting OTD (On-Time Delivery). Quality collaboration: Rapid closure of NCR/8D, reducing PPM (Parts Per Million) and returns. Performance: Drive improvement using metrics such as OTD, PPM, and response time.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 融合Tier-2/3供方在手/在途与产能约束，构建供给可视与缺口预警。 Integrate Tier-2/3 suppliers' on-hand/in-transit inventory and capacity constraints to build supply visibility and gap early warning.
  • 自动从门户/邮件/表格抓取下级供方库存与出货计划，规范化入库。 Automatically capture subordinate suppliers' inventory and shipment plans from portals/emails/spreadsheets, and standardize入库.
  • 对未回传的可视数据/ASN/承诺自动催办并聚合异常清单。 Automatically follow up on unreported visual data/ASN/commitments and aggregate exception lists.
  • 将预测偏差、欠料、产能瓶颈、在途延误进行归因并生成整改单。 Attribute forecast deviations, material shortages, capacity bottlenecks, and in-transit delays, and generate corrective action orders.
  • 结合路线、关务、气象与承运商历史，预测ETA并报警异常。 Predict ETA (Estimated Time of Arrival) and alert anomalies by integrating routes, customs, weather, and carrier history.
  • 展示分层BOM、Tier供方风险、在途/在手、预警与整改状态。 Display layered BOM, Tier supplier risks, in-transit/on-hand status, warnings, and corrective action status.
  • 打通供应商↔料号↔BOM层级的映射关系与变更记录，统一口径。 Connect supplier↔part number↔BOM level mapping relationships and change records to unify standards.
  • 监控各Tier数据交换成功率/延迟/重发率，定位问题伙伴与交易。 Monitor data exchange success rate/delay/retry rate across Tiers to identify problematic partners and transactions.
