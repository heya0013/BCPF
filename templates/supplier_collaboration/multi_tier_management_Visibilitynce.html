多层级供应链管理与可视性

Multi-Tier Supply Management & Visibility
管理全链条供应商层级并实时追踪状态，规避上游信息断层，确保供应链透明度和韧性。 Manage the entire supplier hierarchy and track status in real-time to avoid upstream information gaps, ensuring supply chain transparency and resilience.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

主数据管理
Master Data Management
核心价值 Core Value

协同预测：形成共同需求基线，抑制牛鞭效应，支撑产能/物料；异常治理：避免停线与违约。 Collaborative forecasting: Form a common demand baseline to suppress the bullwhip effect and support capacity/materials; Exception governance: Avoid line stoppages and contract breaches.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 主数据治理（报表+质量规则），建立料号/供应商/地址/Incoterms等主数据校验与变更流程，异常自动报警。 Master data governance (reports + quality rules), establish validation and change processes for master data such as part numbers/suppliers/addresses/Incoterms, with automatic alerts for anomalies.
  • PCN/ECO影响分析（数据分析+NLP），解析PCN文档，映射BOM与替代，量化影响并输出变更排程建议。 PCN/ECO impact analysis (data analysis + NLP), parse PCN documents, map BOM and alternatives, quantify impacts and output change scheduling recommendations.

Smart DOS
核心价值 Core Value

协同预测：形成共同需求基线，抑制牛鞭效应，支撑产能/物料；PO确认：逐项确认作为排产与ASN依据，影响OTD。 Collaborative forecasting: Form a common demand baseline to suppress the bullwhip effect and support capacity/materials; PO confirmation: Item-by-item confirmation as the basis for scheduling and ASN, impacting OTD.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 自动抓取未回签PO，邮件/门户/EDI多通道催办并回写交期/数量变更。 Automatically capture unsigned POs, follow up via email/portal/EDI multiple channels, and write back delivery date/quantity changes.
  • 自动向供方收集RoHS/REACH/冲突矿产等证书并校验有效期，失效前预警。 Automatically collect certificates such as RoHS/REACH/conflict minerals from suppliers and validate validity, with alerts before expiration.
  • 自动校验ASN与PO/装箱单一致性，并触发收货预约与月台排程。 Automatically verify consistency between ASN and PO/packing lists, and trigger receiving appointments and dock scheduling.
  • 聚合预测偏差、承诺违约、运输异常等因素，做归因和优先级建议。 Aggregate factors such as forecast deviations, commitment breaches, and transportation exceptions for attribution and priority recommendations.
  • 利用历史运输、不同时段与清关口径，预测ETA并识别异常轨迹。 Use historical transportation, different time periods, and customs clearance standards to predict ETA and identify abnormal trajectories.
  • 统一OTD/PPM/响应、承诺命中、ASN准确、缺口与分配执行的多维看板。 Unified multi-dimensional dashboard for OTD/PPM/response, commitment hit rate, ASN accuracy, gaps, and allocation execution.
  • 监控事务成功率/延迟/重发率，自动定位异常交易/伙伴。 Monitor transaction success rate/delay/retry rate, automatically locate abnormal transactions/partners.
  • 供方在线自助更新承诺、上传证书、登记变更，RPA校验并入库。 Suppliers self-service online updates to commitments, upload certificates, register changes, with RPA validation and storage.
  • 对接采购/计划/质量邮件与SRM文档，支持自然语言查询承诺、ASN、PCN状态。 Integrate procurement/planning/quality emails and SRM documents, support natural language queries for commitments, ASN, PCN status.
  • EDI/API健康监控（报表+告警），监控事务成功率/延迟/重发率，自动定位异常交易/伙伴。 EDI/API health monitoring (reports + alerts), monitor transaction success rate/delay/retry rate, automatically locate abnormal transactions/partners.

跨站点再平衡
Cross Site Rebalance
核心价值 Core Value

PO确认：逐项确认作为排产与ASN依据，影响OTD；供应风险：地缘/财务/灾害等提前缓释。 PO confirmation: Item-by-item confirmation as the basis for scheduling and ASN, impacting OTD; Supply risk: Early mitigation of geopolitical/financial/disaster risks.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 融合协同预测、产能/物料约束与历史履约，输出承诺与分配建议。 Integrate collaborative forecasting, capacity/material constraints, and historical fulfillment to output commitment and allocation recommendations.
  • 统一 OTD/PPM/响应、承诺命中、ASN准确、缺口与分配执行的多维看板（中的"缺口与分配执行"维度） Unified multi-dimensional dashboard for OTD/PPM/response, commitment hit rate, ASN accuracy, gaps, and allocation execution (including the "gap and allocation execution" dimension).
