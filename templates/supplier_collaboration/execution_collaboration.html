执行协作 Execution Collaboration

Execution Collaboration
聚焦供应链实际落地环节，供应链参与方围绕订单履行、生产进度跟进、物流调度及异常问题（如原材料短缺）处理开展的协同配合，确保前期计划有效落地。 Focusing on the practical implementation of the supply chain, supply chain participants collaborate around order fulfillment, production progress tracking, logistics scheduling, and exception handling (such as raw material shortages) to ensure effective implementation of preliminary plans.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

物料拉动追踪
Material Pull Tracking
关键理由 Key Reason

PO确认：逐项确认交期/数量，作为排产与ASN依据；VMI/寄售：按可视库存与策略补货，缓解滞后与放大；PCN/ECO：提前评估影响与替代，控制停线与呆滞风险。 PO confirmation: Confirm delivery date/quantity item by item as the basis for scheduling and ASN; VMI/Consignment: Replenish based on visible inventory and strategy to alleviate delays and amplification; PCN/ECO: Evaluate impact and alternatives in advance to control line stoppage and obsolescence risks.

物料拉动追踪优化建议： Material Pull Tracking Optimization Recommendations:
  • 质量协同→绩效闭环，把NCR/8D结案自动回写到供应商评分卡，形成持续改进。 Quality collaboration → performance closed-loop, automatically writing back NCR/8D closure to supplier scorecards to form continuous improvement.

预先发货通知（ASN）
Advanced Shipping Notice
关键理由 Key Reason

ASN预报：提前告知到货明细与时间，提升入厂/入库接驳效率；VMI/寄售：按可视库存与策略补货，缓解滞后与放大；PCN/ECO：提前评估影响与替代，控制停线与呆滞风险。 ASN forecast: Inform arrival details and time in advance to improve inbound/docking efficiency; VMI/Consignment: Replenish based on visible inventory and strategy to alleviate delays and amplification; PCN/ECO: Evaluate impact and alternatives in advance to control line stoppage and obsolescence risks.

ASN优化建议： ASN Optimization Recommendations:
  • 短缺分配与加急编排，将承诺不足→allocation→排产/ASN的应急编排标准化与仿真。 Shortage allocation and expedited orchestration, standardizing and simulating emergency orchestration from commitment shortage → allocation → scheduling/ASN.

退货授权（RMA）
Return Merchandise Authorization
关键理由 Key Reason

PO确认：逐项确认交期/数量，作为排产与ASN依据；质量协同（NCR/8D）：快速定责与纠正预防，降低PPM与退货。 PO confirmation: Confirm delivery date/quantity item by item as the basis for scheduling and ASN; Quality collaboration (NCR/8D): Quickly determine responsibility and implement corrective/preventive actions to reduce PPM and returns.

RMA优化建议： RMA Optimization Recommendations:
  • 短缺分配与加急编排，将承诺不足→allocation→排产/ASN的应急编排标准化与仿真。 Shortage allocation and expedited orchestration, standardizing and simulating emergency orchestration from commitment shortage → allocation → scheduling/ASN.
