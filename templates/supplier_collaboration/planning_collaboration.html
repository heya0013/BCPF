Planning Collaboration
优化供应商协作过程中的协作效率，确保各部门无缝衔接，提升供应链整体响应能力与运营效率。 Optimize collaboration efficiency in supplier collaboration processes, ensuring seamless coordination between departments, and enhancing overall supply chain responsiveness and operational efficiency.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

预测协作
Forecast Collaboration
核心价值 Core Value

协同预测：形成共同需求基线，抑制牛鞭效应，支撑产能与物料计划；供应承诺：把可承接能力转为交期/数量承诺，决定缺口与分配策略；PO确认：逐项确认交期/数量，作为排产与ASN依据；库存可视：共享在手/在途库存，支撑补货与分配决策；异常治理：延误/短缺预警与处置，避免停线与违约。 Collaborative forecasting: Establish a common demand baseline to suppress the bullwhip effect and support capacity and material planning; Supply commitment: Convert capacity into delivery/quantity commitments to determine gaps and allocation strategies; PO confirmation: Item-by-item confirmation of delivery dates/quantities as the basis for scheduling and ASN; Inventory visibility: Share on-hand/in-transit inventory to support replenishment and allocation decisions; Exception management: Early warning and handling of delays/shortages to avoid line stoppages and contract breaches.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 预测-产能-承诺一致性引擎，融合CPFR与供给约束，输出可承接量与承诺建议。 Forecast-capacity-commitment consistency engine, integrating CPFR and supply constraints to output achievable volumes and commitment recommendations.
