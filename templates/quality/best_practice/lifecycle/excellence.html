文档/图纸规则抽取
Document/Drawing Rule Extraction

利用自然语言处理和计算机视觉技术自动抽取设计文档和图纸中的规则和约束条件， 确保设计符合行业标准和最佳实践，减少人为解读错误。 Using natural language processing and computer vision technology to automatically extract rules and constraints from design documents and drawings, ensuring designs comply with industry standards and best practices, reducing manual interpretation errors.

  • 规则抽取准确率提升至94%
    Rule extraction accuracy increased to 94%
  • 设计审查时间减少50-65%
    Design review time reduced by 50-65%
  • 标准符合性提高80%
    Standard compliance improved by 80%

BOM与工艺可制造性检查
BOM and Process Manufacturability Check

基于AI算法自动检查物料清单(BOM)和工艺路线的可制造性， 识别潜在的设计问题和制造风险，确保设计方案的可行性。 Based on AI algorithms, automatically checks the manufacturability of bill of materials (BOM) and process routes, identifies potential design issues and manufacturing risks, ensuring design feasibility.

  • 可制造性问题识别率提高70-85%
    Manufacturability issue identification rate improved by 70-85%
  • 设计变更次数减少40-55%
    Design change frequency reduced by 40-55%
  • 制造准备时间缩短30-45%
    Manufacturing preparation time shortened by 30-45%

案例归并与相似性检索
Case Consolidation and Similarity Retrieval

通过机器学习算法自动归并设计案例并实现相似性检索， 快速找到相关设计经验和解决方案，提高设计效率和质量。 Using machine learning algorithms to automatically consolidate design cases and achieve similarity retrieval, quickly finding relevant design experiences and solutions, improving design efficiency and quality.

  • 案例检索准确率提升至90%
    Case retrieval accuracy increased to 90%
  • 设计借鉴效率提高60-75%
    Design reference efficiency improved by 60-75%
  • 重复设计错误减少50-65%
    Repeated design errors reduced by 50-65%

福特汽车 (Ford Motor Company) - AI驱动的设计卓越项目
Ford Motor Company - AI-Driven Design for Excellence Project

福特汽车通过实施基于AIAG APQP/PPAP框架的AI驱动设计卓越解决方案，实现了产品设计流程的数字化转型。 该系统整合了文档规则抽取、BOM可制造性检查和案例相似性检索技术，覆盖全球设计团队和供应商。 Ford Motor Company implemented an AI-driven Design for Excellence solution based on the AIAG APQP/PPAP framework, achieving digital transformation of product design processes. The system integrates document rule extraction, BOM manufacturability checking, and case similarity retrieval technologies, covering global design teams and suppliers.

通过AI驱动的设计优化，福特成功将设计缺陷外溢率降低了35%，DVT/PVT缺陷密度减少了22%， 返修/报废成本降低了40%。系统能够自动识别设计规则冲突，检查BOM与工艺的兼容性， 并通过相似案例检索提供设计改进建议。 Through AI-driven design optimization, Ford successfully reduced design defect spillover rate by 35%, DVT/PVT defect density by 22%, and rework/scrap costs by 40%. The system can automatically identify design rule conflicts, check BOM and process compatibility, and provide design improvement suggestions through similar case retrieval.

关键技术： AIAG APQP/PPAP框架、NLP规则抽取、可制造性分析算法

Key Technologies: AIAG APQP/PPAP Framework, NLP Rule Extraction, Manufacturability Analysis Algorithms
实施范围： 全球5大设计中心，200+供应商，年处理设计项目500+
Implementation Scope: 5 global design centers, 200+ suppliers, annual processing of 500+ design projects
经济效益： 年度节省设计变更和返工成本约$1500万美元
Economic Benefits: Annual savings of approximately $15 million in design changes and rework costs
