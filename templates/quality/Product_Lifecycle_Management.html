产品生命周期管理 Product Lifecycle Management

产品全阶段管理，优化质量相关成本、效率 Manage all stages of the product lifecycle, optimizing quality-related costs and efficiency.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

生产件批准程序 Production Part Approval Process
核心价值 Core Value

以量产条件下的零件与过程文件为对象的合规确认框架，强调能力验证、记录完整与文件一致性。 Provide a compliance confirmation framework focused on parts and process documents under mass production conditions, emphasizing capability verification, complete documentation, and consistency.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 自动抽取能力指数、测量报告、FMEA/CP 一致性，生成 PPAP 包与差异清单。 Automatically extract capability indices, measurement reports, and FMEA/CP consistency to generate PPAP packages and discrepancy lists.
Best Practice
工装验证流程 Tooling Qualification Process
核心价值 Core Value

对量产工装的合格性与稳定性建立验证标准与记录口径，保证批量生产的一致性。 Establish verification standards and recording specifications for the qualification and stability of mass-production tooling to ensure consistency in mass production.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 将工装台账、点检、试模 / 试冲数据与在线 SPC 打通，自动生成资格健康度。 Realize online management of tooling ledgers, inspection data, and trial mold/stamping data; automatically generate qualification health status.
Best Practice
卓越设计
Design for Excellence
核心价值

将可制造性 / 质量 / 可靠性 / 维修性等准则结构化为设计检查项与接受标准，提升早期设计的可生产与可维护性 Structure criteria such as manufacturability, quality, reliability, and maintainability into design check items and acceptance standards to improve the producibility and maintainability of early-stage designs.

优化建议：
  • NCMR（不合格品材料报告）/MRB（物料评审委员会）流转电子化与证据校验，自动生成处置决策建议与追溯要求。 Realize electronic circulation and evidence verification of NCMR (Non-Conforming Material Report)/MRB (Material Review Board); automatically generate disposal decision recommendations and traceability requirements.
Best Practice
流程签核
Process Sign-Off process
核心价值

定义阶段就绪标准与退出准则的制度化机制，确保每个阶段满足预设证据与质量门槛。 An institutionalized mechanism that defines phase readiness criteria and exit criteria to ensure each phase meets preset evidence and quality thresholds.

优化建议：
  • 融合试产 / 样件、服务台、退货、遥测等信号，构建早期告警与应对剧本库。 Integrate signals from trial production/samples, service desks, returns, and telemetry to build an early warning and response script library.
