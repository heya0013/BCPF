供应链质量管理 Supply Chain Quality Management

供应链全链路质量管控，确保物料 / 产品合规达标 Quality control for the entire supply chain to ensure material/product compliance.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

供应商流程变更通知 Supplier Process Change Notification
核心价值 Core Value

规范变更分类、影响评估、记录与版本管理，保证变更受控与可追溯。 Standardize change classification, impact assessment, recording and version management to ensure changes are controlled and traceable.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 搭建 PCN 数字化入口，自动抽取 BOM（物料清单）/ 工艺 / 材料影响范围，联通控制计划与 FMEA（失效模式与影响分析）版本管理。 Establish a digital entry for PCN, automatically extract the impact scope of BOM (Bill of Materials)/processes/materials, and connect control plans with FMEA (Failure Mode and Effects Analysis) version management.

质量审核管理 Quality Audit Management
核心价值 Core Value

定义审计对象、准则与证据要求，形成可比、可度量、可审计的符合性评价体系。 Define audit objects, criteria and evidence requirements to form a comparable, measurable and auditable compliance evaluation system.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 融合审计结果、绩效趋势、PCN（供应商流程变更通知）与质量事件证据，构建动态风险评分与分层治理策略。 Integrate audit results, performance trends, PCN (Supplier Process Change Notification) and quality incident evidence to build a dynamic risk scoring and hierarchical governance strategy.
Best Practice
质量通知与确认
Quality Notification and Confirmation
核心价值

缺陷与不符合的编码与证据体系，要求事实、判定与处置记录完整且一致。 Coding and evidence system for defects and nonconformities, requiring records of facts, judgments and dispositions to be complete and consistent.

优化建议：
  • 质量通知进行自动分流与优先级判定，提供 8D 模板与证据清单，跟踪责任与关闭标准。 Implement automatic classification and priority determination for quality notifications, provide 8D templates and evidence lists, and track responsibilities and closure criteria.
