This process covers key aspects such as generation tracking, product complexity management, and category standardization.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

代际与SBB跟踪
Generation to Generation(G2G) and Active Sales Build Block(SBB) Tracking
核心价值 Core Value

围绕代际（G2G）与SBB的微复杂度与预测闭环跟踪，直接影响BOM标准化、共用率、报价与G2G成本桥，并反哺源头寻源（Source Plan）。在行业最佳实践中，复杂度与标准化被视为寻源与成本优化的前置乘数，平台化的一体化数据贯通可显著提升端到端效率。 Closed-loop tracking of micro-complexity and predictions around Generation to Generation (G2G) and SBB directly impacts BOM standardization, commonality rate, quotation, and G2G cost bridging, and feeds back to source planning. In industry best practices, complexity and standardization are considered pre-multipliers for sourcing and cost optimization, and platform-based integrated data connectivity can significantly improve end-to-end efficiency.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 将G2G/SBB轨迹（规格变化、数量、命中率、资格/费用）统一到可视化看板，生成式AI自动撰写"复杂度-成本/供给"周报/季度洞察与建议行动清单。 Unify G2G/SBB trajectories (spec changes, quantities, hit rates, qualifications/costs) into a visual dashboard, with generative AI automatically creating weekly/quarterly "complexity-cost/supply" insights and recommended action lists.

按产品复杂性管理
By Product Complexity Management
核心价值 Core Value

以MLQ、PCBA/SPEC计数等维度管理"按产品的复杂度"，是控制长尾、提升共用率与降低库存负担的源头抓手；RFQ体量与议价能力亦受其约束，因此属于寻源与成本管理的关键前置。 Managing "complexity by product" through dimensions such as MLQ and PCBA/SPEC count is a fundamental approach to controlling long-tail items, improving commonality rates, and reducing inventory burden; RFQ volume and bargaining power are also constrained by it, making it a key prerequisite for sourcing and cost management.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 把MLQ与库存/交付/价格预测联动，形成MLQ阈值的智能推荐与审批编排（异常闸口+解释），并与RFQ/Source Plan规则一致性校验。 Link MLQ with inventory/delivery/price predictions to form intelligent recommendations and approval workflows for MLQ thresholds (exception gates + explanations), and perform consistency checks with RFQ/Source Plan rules.
  • 以MLQ、SPEC/PCBA计数、长尾分布、资格费用等特征训练"复杂度→成本/交付"的弹性模型，输出削减复杂度的处方式建议（对标件、替代件、量价阈值）。 Train an elastic model of "complexity → cost/delivery" using features such as MLQ, SPEC/PCBA counts, long-tail distribution, and qualification fees, outputting prescriptive recommendations for complexity reduction (benchmark parts, alternative parts, volume-price thresholds).

品类标准化与复杂性管理
Category Standardization and Complexity Management
核心价值 Core Value

面向品类的复杂度度量与规格标准化（Spec Pool）可形成体量集中与共用化，最大化捆绑议价与供应韧性，是端到端S2P中"标准化→寻源→价格/交付"的关键衔接点。 Category-oriented complexity measurement and specification standardization (Spec Pool) can achieve volume concentration and commonality, maximizing bundled bargaining and supply resilience, serving as a key connection point in the end-to-end S2P process from "standardization → sourcing → price/delivery".

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 构建"规格-物料-BOM-品类-供应商"的知识图谱，结合NLP解析SPEC/SBB/MLQ说明，自动识别共用化机会与复杂度驱动因素，形成Spec Pool与共性清单。 Build a knowledge graph of "specification-material-BOM-category-supplier", combine with NLP to parse SPEC/SBB/MLQ descriptions, automatically identify commonality opportunities and complexity drivers, and form Spec Pool and commonality lists.
