寻源管理

Source Management
全面管理寻源流程，从需求分析到风险监控，确保供应连续性、成本优化和合规性。本流程涵盖计划分析、变更管理、寻源指导和风险管理等关键环节。 Comprehensive management of sourcing processes, from demand analysis to risk monitoring, ensuring supply continuity, cost optimization, and compliance. This process covers key aspects such as plan analysis, change management, sourcing guidance, and risk management.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

寻源计划与BOM分析
Source Plan VS Bill of Material(BOM) Analysis
核心价值 Core Value

Source Plan vs BOM Analysis 形成BOM与Source Plan缺口的闭环监控与处置，确保风险缓解动作落地，是量产前后"计划-执行-审计"的关键一公里。 Source Plan vs BOM Analysis forms a closed-loop monitoring and handling of gaps between BOM and Source Plan, ensuring risk mitigation actions are implemented, serving as the critical last mile in the "plan-execution-audit" process before and after mass production.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 将"Source Plan vs BOM"与项目任务盘联动，自动拆解缺口的责任归属与到期项，串接资格/样件/替代件流程，实现例外驱动闭环。 Link "Source Plan vs BOM" with project task boards, automatically decompose responsibility and due items for gaps, connect qualification/sample/alternative part processes, achieving exception-driven closed-loop.

产品变更请求管理
Product Change Request(PCR) Management
核心价值 Core Value

PCR（产品变更）管理在量产与变更阶段决定"替换SPEC/Source、资格与成本/供给"的评估与落地速度，是保障连续供给与成本守门的关键刹车；行业最佳实践强调对PCR影响范围与优先级的快速评估与闭环。 PCR (Product Change Request) management determines the evaluation and implementation speed of "replacing SPEC/Source, qualification, and cost/supply" during mass production and change phases, serving as a critical brake for ensuring continuous supply and cost control; industry best practices emphasize rapid assessment and closed-loop for PCR impact scope and priority.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 构建PCR影响模拟器：自动评估变更影响范围（BOM/供给/价格/资格），按价值与风险排序，并生成处方式建议（替代、资格优先级、量价影响）。 Build a PCR impact simulator: Automatically assess change impact scope (BOM/supply/price/qualification), prioritize by value and risk, and generate prescriptive recommendations (alternatives, qualification priorities, volume-price impact).

寻源指导管理
Source Guidance Management
核心价值 Core Value

Source Guidance 按品类定义多源策略、优先级与数量规则，为RFx事件与项目源头策划提供边界与原则，是寻源一致性与合规的基线。 Source Guidance defines multi-source strategies, priorities, and quantity rules by category, providing boundaries and principles for RFx events and project source planning, serving as the baseline for sourcing consistency and compliance.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 将Source Guidance/风险引擎与RFx平台联动，实现"资格/风险/价格/交期"的多目标综合评分与授标建议，闭环至合同与价目维护。 Link Source Guidance/risk engine with RFx platform to achieve multi-objective comprehensive scoring (qualification/risk/price/delivery) and award recommendations, closing the loop to contract and price list maintenance.

寻源风险管理
Source Risk Management
核心价值 Core Value

Source Risk Management 对新/单/唯/交期/供给等维度持续识别、量化与缓解，提升供应韧性与预警能力；行业趋势转向"连续监测+AI图谱"，将外部与内部风险合并视图化。 Source Risk Management continuously identifies, quantifies, and mitigates risks in new/single/unique/delivery/supply dimensions, enhancing supply resilience and early warning capabilities; industry trends are shifting towards "continuous monitoring + AI knowledge graphs", merging external and internal risks into a unified view.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 构建Source Risk引擎：融合地缘/气候/ESG/财务/网络与内部交付/质量事件，形成多层KRI与预警；将风险与Source Guidance阈值联动。 Build a Source Risk engine: Integrate geopolitical/climate/ESG/financial/cyber risks with internal delivery/quality events to form multi-layer KRIs and alerts; link risks with Source Guidance thresholds.
  • 在关键品类上定期运行"断供/交期拉长/资格失效"的情景演练，驱动多源/替代/缓冲策略的优化，并与Source Guidance固化为标准。 Regularly conduct scenario drills for "supply disruption/delivery extension/qualification failure" in key categories, driving optimization of multi-source/alternative/buffer strategies, and solidifying them into Source Guidance standards.
