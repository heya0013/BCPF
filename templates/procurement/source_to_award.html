寻源到授标流程

Source to Award
从需求识别到供应商选择的完整流程，确保采购活动高效、合规且最大化价值。本流程涵盖RFQ管理、本地寻源、授权管理及寻源策略制定等关键环节。 A complete process from demand identification to supplier selection, ensuring efficient, compliant, and value-maximizing procurement activities. This process covers key aspects such as RFQ management, local sourcing, authorization management, and sourcing strategy development.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

RFQ管理
Requests for Quotation (RFQ) Management
核心价值 Core Value

RFQ/招标管理是"从需求到授标"的核心执行环节，直接影响命中率、采购周期与价格发现效率；行业常以平台化与算法择优（评分/加权）提升透明度与合规性。 RFQ/bidding management is the core execution link in the "demand to award" process, directly impacting hit rate, procurement cycle, and price discovery efficiency; the industry often uses platformization and algorithmic optimization (scoring/weighting) to enhance transparency and compliance.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • RFx自动化/评分模型，建立多维评分（价格/交期/质量/风险/ESG）与权重可配置模型，自动汇总评标并生成授标建议与解释。 RFx automation/scoring model, establish a multi-dimensional scoring (price/delivery/quality/risk/ESG) and configurable weighting model, automatically summarize bids and generate award recommendations and explanations.

本地寻源
Local Source
核心价值 Core Value

本地化/Local Source 兼顾关税、交付时效、ESG与政策要求，是在关键市场快速响应与韧性建设的重要抓手；需要与全局策略/资格规则协同。 Localization/Local Source balances tariffs, delivery timeliness, ESG, and policy requirements, serving as a key lever for rapid response and resilience building in critical markets; requires coordination with global strategies/qualification rules.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 本地化寻源加速，以本地税则/交期/资质为特征构建本地化候选库与优先级，自动生成本地化方案与财务影响测算。 Local sourcing acceleration, build a localized candidate database and priority based on local tax rules/delivery/qualifications, automatically generate localization solutions and financial impact calculations.

授权与内部自评管理
Delegation and Internal Self-Assessment Management
核心价值 Core Value

DOA授权与内部自评保障流程与授标合规、权责清晰，可显著降低审计/舞弊风险，并提升审批效率与一致性。 DOA authorization and internal self-assessment ensure process and award compliance, clear responsibilities, significantly reducing audit/fraud risks, and improving approval efficiency and consistency.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 将DOA与流程节点绑定，异常越权与红旗自动预警；自评报告由生成式AI自动撰写并分派整改。 Bind DOA with process nodes, automatically warn of abnormal overstepping and red flags; self-assessment reports are automatically written by generative AI and assigned for rectification.

寻源策略管理
Sourcing Strategy Management
核心价值 Core Value

类目/品类寻源策略（多源、份额、授标规则）提供RFx的"方法-边界-阈值"，是成本、交期、风险与合规的上游设计，决定授标质量与稳定性。 Category sourcing strategies (multi-source, share, award rules) provide the "method-boundary-threshold" for RFx, serving as the upstream design for cost, delivery, risk, and compliance, determining award quality and stability.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 将授标结果与执行数据（量/价/履约）回流，自动校准品类策略的多源份额与阈值。 Feed back award results and execution data (volume/price/performance) to automatically calibrate multi-source shares and thresholds for category strategies.
