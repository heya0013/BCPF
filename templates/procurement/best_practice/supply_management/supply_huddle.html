AI应用场景 AI Application Scenarios

事件融合模型 Event Fusion Model

整合多源供应链数据（库存、运输、生产等），构建统一的事件视图，实现供应链状态的全面感知。 Integrating multi-source supply chain data (inventory, transportation, production, etc.) to build a unified event view and achieve comprehensive perception of supply chain status.

异常检测 Anomaly Detection

利用机器学习算法实时识别供应链异常情况，提前预警潜在风险，缩短问题响应时间。 Using machine learning algorithms to identify supply chain anomalies in real time, providing early warnings of potential risks, and shortening problem response time.

LLM会议纪要/行动清单生成 LLM Meeting Minutes/Action List Generation

基于大语言模型自动生成会议纪要和行动清单，确保会议成果有效跟踪和执行。 Automatically generating meeting minutes and action lists based on large language models to ensure effective tracking and execution of meeting outcomes.

By implementing AI-driven Supply Huddle solutions, enterprises can achieve the following key improvements:

异常响应效率提升 Exception Response Efficiency Improvement

AI异常检测系统能够实时识别问题并自动分配责任人，大幅缩短异常关闭周期。 AI anomaly detection systems can identify problems in real time and automatically assign responsible persons, significantly shortening the exception closure cycle.

跨部门协作优化 Cross-departmental Collaboration Optimization

事件融合模型打破部门信息孤岛，实现供应链全链路可视化，加速跨部门决策流程。 Event fusion models break down departmental information silos, achieve end-to-end supply chain visibility, and accelerate cross-departmental decision-making processes.

AI自动生成会议纪要和行动清单，减少人工记录时间，确保会议成果有效落实。 AI automatically generates meeting minutes and action lists, reducing manual recording time and ensuring effective implementation of meeting outcomes.

实际行业案例 Real Industry Case

神州控股科捷供应链协作平台案例 Digital China Kejie Supply Chain Collaboration Platform Case

神州控股科捷在其昆山旗舰仓实施了AI驱动的供应链协作平台，通过事件融合模型和异常检测技术，显著提升了供应链例会的效率和效果。 Digital China Kejie implemented an AI-driven supply chain collaboration platform at its Kunshan flagship warehouse, significantly improving the efficiency and effectiveness of supply chain meetings through event fusion models and anomaly detection technologies.

实施效果：异常关闭周期缩短22%，跨部门响应时间减少28%，会议准备时间降低65%。 Implementation results: Exception closure cycle shortened by 22%, cross-departmental response time reduced by 28%, and meeting preparation time decreased by 65%.

案例出处：基于神州控股科捷的"AI for Process"实践，该平台通过数字化看板实现供应链全链路可视化，AI算法实时监控各环节状态并自动预警异常。 Case source: Based on Digital China Kejie's "AI for Process" practice, this platform achieves end-to-end supply chain visibility through digital dashboards, with AI algorithms monitoring the status of each link in real time and automatically warning of anomalies.
