Automatic Obligation Extraction

Intelligent Event Monitoring

实时监控供应链事件，自动识别潜在中断风险并提前预警 Real-time monitoring of supply chain events, automatically identifying potential disruption risks and providing early warnings

Automated Task Orchestration

根据合同义务和事件状态，自动生成并分配任务给相关人员 Automatically generating and assigning tasks to relevant personnel based on contract obligations and event status
