Bid Evaluation Model

基于机器学习算法自动评估投标方案，综合考虑价格、技术、商务和服务等多个维度 Using machine learning algorithms to automatically evaluate bid proposals, comprehensively considering multiple dimensions such as price, technology, business and service

  • 评标准确率提升至92% Bid evaluation accuracy increased to 92%
  • 评标时间缩短65-75% Bid evaluation time reduced by 65-75%

生成式解释

Generative Explanation

利用大语言模型自动生成采购决策解释和评标报告，提升决策透明度和可追溯性 Using large language models to automatically generate procurement decision explanations and bid evaluation reports, improving decision transparency and traceability

  • 报告生成时间缩短75-85% Report generation time reduced by 75-85%
  • 解释质量提升40-50% Explanation quality improved by 40-50%
