多目标评分

Multi-objective Scoring

利用AI算法对供应商报价进行多维度综合评分，平衡价格、质量、交付周期等关键因素 Using AI algorithms to comprehensively score supplier quotes across multiple dimensions, balancing price, quality, delivery time and other key factors

  • 评分准确率提升至94% Scoring accuracy increased to 94%
  • 决策时间缩短65-75% Decision time reduced by 65-75%

异常检测

Anomaly Detection

基于机器学习识别异常报价模式，检测潜在价格操纵或错误报价 Using machine learning to identify abnormal quotation patterns, detecting potential price manipulation or erroneous quotes

  • 异常检测准确率提升至90% Anomaly detection accuracy increased to 90%
  • 风险识别时间缩短70-85% Risk identification time reduced by 70-85%

生成式解释

Generative Explanation

利用生成式AI自动生成授标决策解释，提高决策透明度和供应商接受度 Using generative AI to automatically generate explanations for award decisions, improving transparency and supplier acceptance

  • 解释质量提升45-55% Explanation quality improved by 45-55%
  • 供应商争议减少40-50% Supplier disputes reduced by 40-50%
