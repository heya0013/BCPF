Cross-Site Inventory Rebalance Optimization

基于AI驱动的需求预测、库存优化和智能调拨决策技术，实现多仓库库存动态平衡 AI-driven demand forecasting, inventory optimization, and intelligent transfer decisions to achieve dynamic inventory balance across multiple warehouses

需求预测引擎

Demand Forecasting Engine

基于机器学习和时间序列分析，预测各站点的未来需求，考虑季节性、促销活动和市场趋势 Predict future demand at each site using machine learning and time series analysis, considering seasonality, promotions, and market trends

  • 预测准确率提升至95%+ Forecast accuracy increased to 95%+
  • 需求波动响应时间缩短60% 60% reduction in demand fluctuation response time
  • 支持多层级预测（SKU、品类、区域） Support multi-level forecasting (SKU, category, region)

库存优化算法

Inventory Optimization Algorithm

实时计算最优库存水平和安全库存阈值，平衡服务水平与库存成本 Calculate optimal inventory levels and safety stock thresholds in real-time, balancing service levels and inventory costs

  • 库存周转率提升15-25% Inventory turnover rate increased by 15-25%
  • 库存持有成本降低20-30% Inventory holding costs reduced by 20-30%
  • 缺货率降低30-40% Out-of-stock rate reduced by 30-40%

智能调拨决策系统

Intelligent Transfer Decision System

基于库存状态和需求预测，自动生成最优调拨方案，考虑运输成本、时效和约束条件 Automatically generate optimal transfer plans based on inventory status and demand forecasts, considering transportation costs, timeliness, and constraints

  • 调拨决策时间缩短80% 80% reduction in transfer decision time
  • 运输成本降低10-20% Transportation costs reduced by 10-20%
  • 紧急调拨需求减少35-45% Emergency transfer requirements reduced by 35-45%
