采购申请与采购订单管理

PR and PO Management
优化采购申请和订单处理流程，提高审批效率，缩短采购周期，降低合规风险。本流程涵盖PR创建、PO生成、审批工作流等关键环节，确保采购流程高效、规范。 Optimize purchase requisition and order processing workflows, improve approval efficiency, shorten procurement cycles, and reduce compliance risks. This process covers key aspects such as PR creation, PO generation, and approval workflows, ensuring efficient and standardized procurement processes.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

采购订单管理
PO Management
核心价值 Core Value

PO管理贯穿"请购→下单→收货→对账"的执行主线，是交付达成率、发票准确度与库存周转的核心抓手；行业最佳实践强调异常闭环（延期/欠交/价格不一致）与端到端可追溯。 PO management runs through the execution chain of "requisition → ordering → receipt → reconciliation", serving as the core driver for delivery achievement rate, invoice accuracy, and inventory turnover; industry best practices emphasize closed-loop exception handling (delays/shortages/price discrepancies) and end-to-end traceability.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 异常预测/智能催交 Exception Prediction/Intelligent Expediting
  • 构建PO异常预测模型（延期/欠交/价格不一致），自动触发催交/改约/替代建议，并生成异常解释与影响评估。 Build PO exception prediction models (delays/shortages/price discrepancies), automatically trigger expediting/rescheduling/substitution recommendations, and generate exception explanations and impact assessments.
  • 按规则从预测/补货/合同触发PO草案，自动填充主数据与条款并路由审批；异常项（缺字段/价差/黑名单）自动拦截。 Trigger PO drafts from forecasts/replenishment/contracts based on rules, automatically populate master data and terms, and route for approval; automatically intercept exceptions (missing fields/price differences/blacklist).

采购主数据管理
Purchasing Master Data Management
核心价值 Core Value

采购主数据（物料/供应商/地点/价格条件等）是PR/PO/收货/发票的单据口径与校验基座；高质量主数据可显著降低价格/品类/地点错误导致的返工与争议。 Purchasing master data (materials/suppliers/locations/price terms, etc.) forms the basis for document standards and validation of PR/PO/receipt/invoice; high-quality master data significantly reduces rework and disputes caused by price/category/location errors.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 建立"主数据质量仪表盘"（必填/唯一/一致/时效），对影响PR/PO的关键字段（价格条件、地点、供应商代码、税率）做自动校验与修复工单。 Establish a "master data quality dashboard" (mandatory/unique/consistent/timely), perform automatic validation and create repair tickets for key fields affecting PR/PO (price terms, locations, supplier codes, tax rates).

预采
Buy Ahead
核心价值 Core Value

Buy Ahead（提前备料/预占LOI）在供需紧张或长交期场景下平衡风险与资金占用，需与预测/交期/库存策略联动，避免积压与呆滞。 Buy Ahead (advance material preparation/pre-allocation LOI) balances risks and capital occupation in tight supply-demand or long lead-time scenarios, requiring linkage with forecasts/lead times/inventory strategies to avoid backlog and obsolescence.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 将供需预测、交期、价格趋势与资金成本纳入目标函数，给出 Buy Ahead 额度/周期/覆盖对象建议，并跟踪实际收益与库存影响。 Incorporate supply-demand forecasts, lead times, price trends, and capital costs into the objective function to provide Buy Ahead quota/cycle/coverage recommendations, and track actual benefits and inventory impact.
