供应管理

Supply Management
优化供应链管理，确保物料及时供应并降低库存成本，提高供应链韧性。本流程涵盖供应商选择、库存控制、物流协调等关键环节，帮助企业建立高效、可靠的供应体系。 Optimize supply chain management to ensure timely material supply, reduce inventory costs, and enhance supply chain resilience. This process covers key aspects such as supplier selection, inventory control, and logistics coordination, helping enterprises establish efficient and reliable supply systems.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

数字化例会 Digital Meeting
Supply Huddle
关键理由 Key Reason

Supply Huddle(数字化例会)把在途/库存/异常与计划偏差拉通，形成"问题清单—责任—进展"的可视闭环，是落地 S&OP 决策与推动执行的主场。 Supply Huddle (digital meeting) integrates in-transit/inventory/exceptions and plan deviations, forming a visible closed loop of "issue list-responsibility-progress", serving as the main platform for implementing S&OP decisions and driving execution.

供应协调会议优化建议： Supply Coordination Meeting Optimization Recommendations:
  • 数字化 Supply Huddle(问题编排) Digital Supply Huddle (Issue Orchestration)
  • 汇聚 PO/ASN/库存/Commit/异常为一张事实表，自动生成"问题清单+责任+到期日"并驱动跨部门跟踪，例会直接消费数据与剧本。 Integrate PO/ASN/inventory/Commit/exceptions into a single fact table, automatically generate "issue list + responsibility + due date" and drive cross-departmental tracking, with meetings directly consuming data and scripts.

区域产品S&OP
Regional Product S&OP
关键理由 Key Reason

区域产品S&OP通过整合销售预测与运营规划，确保供需平衡，减少库存积压，提高资金周转率，同时增强对区域市场变化的快速响应能力。 Regional Product S&OP integrates sales forecasts and operational planning to ensure supply-demand balance, reduce inventory backlog, improve capital turnover, and enhance rapid response capabilities to regional market changes.

区域产品S&OP优化建议: Regional Product S&OP Optimization Recommendations:
  • 区域 S&OP 预测与对齐 Regional S&OP Forecasting and Alignment
  • 用分层时序+共识算法提升区域层面的需求/供应一致性,对齐滚动承诺并推送异常偏差(MAPE/偏差热力)。 Use hierarchical time series + consensus algorithms to improve demand/supply consistency at the regional level, align rolling commitments, and push exception deviations (MAPE/deviation heatmap).

ODM直购订单与库存审计
ODM Direct Buy(DB) PO and Stock Audit
关键理由 Key Reason

ODM DB PO 与库存审计保障"按LT下单、按节奏备货、凭据齐全"，通过自动化抓取与比对确保计划口径、物流记录与凭证一致，降低合规与断供风险。 ODM DB PO and stock audit ensure "ordering according to LT, stocking according to rhythm, and complete documentation", reducing compliance and supply disruption risks through automated capture and comparison to ensure consistency between planning, logistics records, and vouchers.

ODM直购订单与库存审计优化建议： ODM Direct Buy Order and Stock Audit Optimization Recommendations:
  • 建立标准化的ODM直购订单审批流程，缩短处理时间 Establish a standardized ODM direct buy order approval process to shorten processing time
  • 实施自动化订单匹配系统，减少人工操作错误 Implement an automated order matching system to reduce manual operation errors
  • 开发智能库存审计工具，实现实时库存可视化 Develop intelligent inventory audit tools to achieve real-time inventory visualization
  • 建立供应商库存管理协作平台，提高库存透明度 Establish a supplier inventory management collaboration platform to improve inventory transparency
  • 实施风险预警系统，自动识别异常订单和库存变动 Implement a risk early warning system to automatically identify abnormal orders and inventory changes
  • 制定ODM供应商绩效评估体系，定期审计供应商合规性 Develop an ODM supplier performance evaluation system to regularly audit supplier compliance
