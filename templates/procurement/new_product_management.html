新产品管理

New Product Management
全面管理新产品引入流程，从寻源计划到产品组合跟踪，确保新产品按时高质量上市。本流程涵盖多源策略、RFQ命中率、NPI管理和代际产品跟踪等关键环节。 Comprehensive management of new product introduction process, from sourcing plan to product portfolio tracking, ensuring new products are launched on time with high quality. This process covers key aspects such as multi-source strategy, RFQ hit rate, NPI management and generational product tracking.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

寻源计划管理
Source Plan Management
核心价值 Core Value

NPI阶段的多源策略（数量、优先级、地域/交期）直接决定成本/交付风险与BOM可制造性，是打通RFQ→合同→PO的上游主开关；行业最佳实践强调"及早介入采购+长交期件前置策划+多源容错"。 The multi-source strategy (quantity, priority, region/delivery) during the NPI phase directly determines cost/delivery risks and BOM manufacturability, serving as the upstream master switch connecting RFQ → contract → PO. Industry best practices emphasize "early procurement involvement + long-lead item pre-planning + multi-source fault tolerance".

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 对工程BOM与Sourcing数据进行一致性校验（关键件二供覆盖、替代策略、资格状态与Gate匹配），自动出具"缺口清单+闭环责任"。 Perform consistency checks on engineering BOM and sourcing data (secondary source coverage for critical parts, alternative strategies, qualification status and gate matching), automatically generating "gap list + closed-loop responsibility".
  • 基于需求预测、供给能力、资格/交期/地域风险，自动给出多源数量与优先级建议（含情景仿真），并按RFQ命中/实际量回流校准Source Plan。 Based on demand forecasting, supply capacity, qualification/delivery/regional risks, automatically provide multi-source quantity and priority recommendations (including scenario simulation), and calibrate the Source Plan based on RFQ hit/actual volume feedback.

RFQ命中率管理
Product & Parts Request for Quotation(RFQ) Hit Rate
核心价值 Core Value

RFQ命中率衡量从报价到实际量的转化质量，反映需求预测、供应匹配与成本/交付能力的综合水平，是NPI与寻源闭环的重要KPI类目。 RFQ hit rate measures the conversion quality from quotation to actual volume, reflecting the comprehensive level of demand forecasting, supply matching and cost/delivery capability, serving as an important KPI category for NPI and sourcing closed-loop.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 将"报价量→中标量→实际量"串接，自动拆解命中率变化的成因（价格、交期、资格、复杂度），生成改善建议与"下轮RFQ策略"。 Connect "quoted volume → awarded volume → actual volume" in series, automatically decompose the causes of hit rate changes (price, delivery, qualification, complexity), and generate improvement suggestions and "next-round RFQ strategy".

新产品引入管理
ISG New Product Introduction(NPI)
核心价值 Core Value

NPI流程（含API跟踪与特定客户场景流程）确保"全件到位与按时量产"的节奏控制，是TTM与首批次质量/交付的基础保障。 The NPI process (including API tracking and specific customer scenario processes) ensures rhythm control of "all parts in place and mass production on time", serving as the fundamental guarantee for TTM and first-batch quality/delivery.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 对BOM零件按交期/资格/供应画像构建API（All-Parts-In）风险评分，自动生成"提前下单/替代件/加速资格"的处方式建议，并在NPI里程碑前滚动预警。 Build API (All-Parts-In) risk scoring for BOM parts based on delivery/qualification/supply profile, automatically generate prescriptive recommendations for "early ordering/alternative parts/accelerated qualification", and provide rolling alerts before NPI milestones.

代际产品组合跟踪
Generation to Generation(G2G) Product Portfolio Tracking
核心价值 Core Value

G2G产品组合跟踪为代际转移、产量与生产力变化提供可视化输入，支撑成本桥与转产风险管理，是NPI组合治理与资源配置的关键度量。 G2G product portfolio tracking provides visual input for generational transitions, production volume and productivity changes, supports cost bridging and production transfer risk management, serving as a key metric for NPI portfolio governance and resource allocation.

优化建议： Optimization Recommendations:
  • 把G2G组合、功能变化与物料层面的成本桥联动展示，结合外部MPN成本对标识别"超支与机会"并形成NPI降本路线图。 Display G2G portfolio, functional changes and material-level cost bridges in an interconnected manner, identify "overruns and opportunities" through external MPN cost benchmarking, and form an NPI cost reduction roadmap.
