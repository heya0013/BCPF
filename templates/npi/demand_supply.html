统筹新产品的需求预测、供应规划，确保供需匹配 Coordinate new product demand forecasting and supply planning to ensure supply-demand matching.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

产能管理 Capacity Management
核心价值 Core Value

建立产能模型（工序/设备/班次/良率/稼动/切换）与时间窗规则，形成能力度量与承诺边界的统一口径。 Establish a capacity model (processes, equipment, shifts, yield, OEE, changeover) and time window rules to form unified standards for capacity measurement and commitment boundaries.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 建立工序 / 设备 / 班次 / 良率 / 切换的产能模型与情景仿真，输出放量窗口与约束解耦策略。 Build capacity model & scenario simulation (process/equipment/shift/yield/changeover), output ramp-up window & constraint decoupling strategy.

PSS 管理 PSS Management
核心价值 Core Value

定义可出货供给（PSS）的计量单位、时间窗与里程碑（POR→SLE），形成对市场承诺的供给基线与审计证据。 Define the measurement units, time windows and milestones (POR→SLE) for shippable supply (PSS) to form a supply baseline and audit evidence for market commitments.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 将 PSS 定义的单位、时间窗、门限与缺口算法标准化，形成市场承诺与供给基线的一致呈现 Standardize PSS units, time windows, thresholds & gap algorithms; form consistent presentation of market commitment and supply baseline.

工装管理（ODM）
Tooling Management (ODM)
核心价值

对复制工装的数量、配置、合格性与可用率建立标准与记录口径，保障冗余能力与批量一致性。。 Establish standards and recording criteria for the quantity, configuration, qualification, and availability of duplicate tooling to ensure redundancy capability and batch consistency.

优化建议：
  • 建立复制工装数量 / 寿命 / 能力 / 可用率的健康评分，联动产能模型生成冗余与备份策略。 Establish health score for duplicate tooling (quantity/life/capacity/availability); link to capacity model to generate redundancy & backup strategies.

API 管理
API Management
核心价值

建立量产在管零部件清单与状态编码（合格/待定/替代/停用等），确保BOM覆盖与物料可用性的判定口径一致。 Establish a managed parts list and status codes (qualified/pending/alternative/discontinued, etc.) for mass production to ensure consistent judgment criteria for BOM coverage and material availability.

优化建议：
  • 标准化在管件状态码、就绪属性与替代关系；以 BOM 覆盖与在途可用性驱动短缺预警。 Standardize in-transit component status codes, readiness attributes & alternatives; drive shortage alerts via BOM coverage and in-transit availability.

SCRR 管理
SCRR Management
核心价值

建立供应链就绪度评审（SCRR）的条目库、判定标准与证据清单，统一风险分级与整改闭环要求。 Establish the item library, assessment criteria and evidence list for Supply Chain Readiness Review (SCRR), and standardize requirements for risk classification and rectification closed-loop.

优化建议：
  • 对 SCRR 条目进行证据自动抽取与合规校验，生成缺口清单与整改跟踪，按风险分级监控闭环。 Conduct automatic evidence extraction & compliance verification for SCRR items; generate gap list & rectification tracking; implement risk-based graded monitoring and closed loop.
