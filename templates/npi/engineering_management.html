新产品工程管理 NP Engineering Management

统筹新产品工程设计、工艺落地及设备适配等相关工作。 Coordinate work related to new product engineering design, process implementation and equipment adaptation.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

SCER 管理 SCER Management
核心价值 Core Value

形成规则、度量与证据的标准体系，确保过程一致、结果可比、全程可审计. Establish a standard system for rules, metrics and evidence to ensure consistent processes, comparable results and full-process auditability.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 引入物料/产能/换型/在途ETA约束的联合校验，形成计划可执行性评分与调整建议。 Introduce joint verification of constraints including materials, capacity, changeover, and in-transit ETA to generate plan feasibility scores and adjustment suggestions.
  • 基于客户/区域/利润/承诺权重的优先级模型，输出分配与回退规则，保留审计轨迹。 Based on a priority model weighted by customer, region, profit, and commitment, output allocation and rollback rules while retaining audit trails.
  • 结合BOM覆盖、在途不确定性与良率波动预测缺料风险；形成加急阈值与干预剧本。 Predict material shortage risks by integrating BOM coverage, in-transit uncertainty, and yield fluctuations; establish urgency thresholds and intervention scenarios.
  • 长交期物料按影响/概率分层；对供应/物流/地缘风险建立提前锁料与替代路径。 Stratify long-lead-time materials by impact and probability; establish advance material locking and alternative paths for supply, logistics, and geopolitical risks.
