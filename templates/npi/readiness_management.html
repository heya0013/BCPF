新产品就绪管理 NP Readiness Management

统筹新产品量产前各项准备工作，核查推进并管控风险，确保满足量产条件。 Coordinate preparatory work for new products before mass production, verify progress and control risks to meet mass production requirements.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

试产、爬坡及量产启动管理 Pilot&Ramp&Launch Management
核心价值 Core Value

定义样机/试产/量产前阶段的就绪标准与验证证据口径，确保阶段退出具备客观依据。 Define the readiness criteria and specifications for verification evidence for the prototype, pilot production and pre-mass production stages to ensure that the stage exit is supported by objective evidence.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 建立工序 / 设备 / 班次 / 良率 / 切换的产能模型与情景仿真，输出放量窗口与约束解耦策略。 Build capacity model & scenario simulation (process/equipment/shift/yield/changeover), output ramp-up window & constraint decoupling strategy.
Best Practice
技术认可，首件检验与样品认可管理 TA_FAI_SA Management
核心价值 Core Value

明确首件/初件报告的样本、量测与证据结构，作为批准的重要依据。 Clarify the sample information, measurement details and evidence structure of the first article report, which shall serve as an important basis for approval.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 将控制计划、FMEA 交付物结构化与机读化，执行完整性、版本映射校验。 Structure and digitize deliverables such as Control Plan and FMEA, and perform integrity and version mapping verification.
Best Practice
海外工厂赋能
Oversea Factory Enablement
核心价值

形成规则、度量与证据的标准体系，确保过程一致、结果可比、全程可审计。 Establish a standardized system of rules, metrics and evidence to ensure process consistency, result comparability and full-process auditability.

优化建议：
  • 构建可维护的 DFx 规则库，配套海外工厂落地培训，实现对 BOM / 图纸 / 工艺的标准化静态检查。 Establish a maintainable DFx rule library, provide supporting landing training for overseas factories, and realize standardized static inspections on BOM/drawings/processes.
Best Practice
生产需求评审
Build Request review
核心价值

建立量产在管零部件清单与状态编码（合格/待定/替代/停用等），确保BOM覆盖与物料可用性的判定口径一致。 Establish a managed parts list and status codes (qualified/pending/alternative/discontinued, etc.) for mass production to ensure consistent judgment criteria for BOM coverage and material availability.

优化建议：
  • 自动抽取变更影响的零部件、工艺与文件，生成影响清单与同步任务，监控 PPAP/PSO/BOM 冻结一致性。 Automatically extract components, processes and documents affected by changes, generate impact lists and synchronization tasks, and monitor the freeze consistency of PPAP/PSO/BOM.
