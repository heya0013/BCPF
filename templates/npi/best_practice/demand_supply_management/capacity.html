AI应用场景
AI Application Scenarios

时序预测
Time Series Forecasting

利用时间序列预测模型，准确预测设备运行状态、产能需求和维护周期，实现产能规划的精准预测和主动调整，提高设备可用率和生产效率。 Using time series forecasting models to accurately predict equipment operating status, capacity demand and maintenance cycles, achieving precise capacity planning and proactive adjustment, improving equipment availability and production efficiency.

离散事件仿真
Discrete Event Simulation

通过离散事件仿真技术，模拟生产流程中的复杂交互和随机事件，优化生产排程和资源分配，提高系统产出率和资源利用率。 Using discrete event simulation technology to simulate complex interactions and random events in production processes, optimizing production scheduling and resource allocation, improving system throughput and resource utilization.

瓶颈识别与策略推荐
Bottleneck Identification and Strategy Recommendation

利用机器学习算法自动识别生产系统中的瓶颈环节，提供针对性的优化策略和改进建议，最大化瓶颈资源利用率和整体产出效率。 Using machine learning algorithms to automatically identify bottleneck links in production systems, providing targeted optimization strategies and improvement suggestions, maximizing bottleneck resource utilization and overall output efficiency.

通过AI驱动的Capacity Management优化解决方案，企业能够显著提高瓶颈资源利用率和产出率。时序预测技术提供了对设备状态和产能需求的精准预测能力，离散事件仿真技术则优化了生产流程和资源分配，瓶颈识别与策略推荐技术自动识别生产瓶颈并提供优化建议，最大化设备利用率和生产效率。 Through AI-driven Capacity Management optimization solutions, enterprises can significantly improve bottleneck resource utilization and throughput. Time series forecasting technology provides accurate prediction capabilities for equipment status and capacity demand, discrete event simulation technology optimizes production processes and resource allocation, and bottleneck identification and strategy recommendation technology automatically identifies production bottlenecks and provides optimization suggestions, maximizing equipment utilization and production efficiency.

AI技术通过分析历史生产数据、设备运行状态和产能需求，识别最优生产模式和最佳实践，提供个性化的产能规划建议和决策支持。离散事件仿真技术考虑了多种动态因素，包括设备故障、物料供应和生产优先级，生成最优的生产排程和资源分配方案，最大化产出率和资源利用率。结合SEMI E10设备状态与可用率和SEMI E79产出/效率指标的最佳实践，确保Capacity Management流程的准确性和一致性。 AI technology analyzes historical production data, equipment operating status and capacity demand to identify optimal production patterns and best practices, providing personalized capacity planning suggestions and decision support. Discrete event simulation technology considers multiple dynamic factors, including equipment failure, material supply and production priority, to generate optimal production scheduling and resource allocation solutions, maximizing throughput and resource utilization. Combined with SEMI E10 Equipment State and Availability and SEMI E79 Output/Efficiency Metrics best practices to ensure the accuracy and consistency of Capacity Management processes.

台积电作为全球领先的半导体制造企业，通过实施AI驱动的Capacity Management系统，实现了生产产能的智能化管理和优化。该系统基于时序预测、离散事件仿真和瓶颈识别技术，为晶圆制造提供全面的产能规划和优化解决方案。 As a global leading semiconductor manufacturing enterprise, TSMC implemented an AI-driven Capacity Management system, achieving intelligent management and optimization of production capacity. The system is based on time series forecasting, discrete event simulation and bottleneck identification technologies, providing comprehensive capacity planning and optimization solutions for wafer manufacturing.

通过实施AI驱动的Capacity Management系统，台积电成功将瓶颈资源利用率提高了8个百分点，产出率提高了12%，设备可用率提高了9%，生产周期缩短了25%。系统能够精准预测设备状态和产能需求，智能仿真生产流程，自动识别生产瓶颈，并提供详细的优化策略和改进建议，大幅提高了生产效率和资源利用率。 By implementing the AI-driven Capacity Management system, TSMC successfully increased bottleneck resource utilization by 8 percentage points, throughput by 12%, equipment availability by 9%, and reduced production cycle by 25%. The system can accurately predict equipment status and capacity demand, intelligently simulate production processes, automatically identify production bottlenecks, and provide detailed optimization strategies and improvement suggestions, significantly improving production efficiency and resource utilization.
