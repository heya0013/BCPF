AI Application Scenarios

缺口预测
Gap Prediction

利用机器学习算法预测产品供应中的潜在缺口和短缺风险，实现早期预警和主动干预，提高供应稳定性和可出货率。 Using machine learning algorithms to predict potential gaps and shortage risks in product supply, achieving early warning and proactive intervention, improving supply stability and shippable rate.

供需匹配优化
Supply-Demand Matching Optimization

通过优化算法智能匹配供应能力和市场需求，动态调整分配策略，最大化资源利用率和客户满意度。 Using optimization algorithms to intelligently match supply capacity and market demand, dynamically adjusting allocation strategies, maximizing resource utilization and customer satisfaction.

可视化预警
Visual Early Warning

利用数据可视化技术实时监控供应状态和风险指标，提供直观的预警界面和决策支持，提高响应速度和问题解决效率。 Using data visualization technology to monitor supply status and risk indicators in real-time, providing intuitive early warning interfaces and decision support, improving response speed and problem-solving efficiency.

联合利华作为全球领先的快速消费品公司，通过实施AI驱动的PSS管理系统，实现了产品供应服务的智能化管理和优化。该系统基于缺口预测、供需匹配优化和可视化预警技术，为全球供应链网络提供全面的供应服务解决方案。 As a global leading fast-moving consumer goods company, Unilever implemented an AI-driven PSS management system, achieving intelligent management and optimization of product supply services. The system is based on gap prediction, supply-demand matching optimization and visual early warning technologies, providing comprehensive supply service solutions for global supply chain networks.

通过实施AI驱动的PSS管理系统，联合利华成功将按期可出货率提高了18个百分点，按期交付率提高了12个百分点，供应缺口减少了32%，响应效率提升了38%。系统能够智能预测供应缺口，优化供需匹配策略，实时监控供应状态，并提供详细的预警信息和决策支持，大幅提高了产品供应服务的稳定性和响应能力。 By implementing the AI-driven PSS management system, Unilever successfully increased on-time shippable supply rate by 18 percentage points, on-time delivery rate by 12 percentage points, reduced supply gaps by 32%, and improved response efficiency by 38%. The system can intelligently predict supply gaps, optimize supply-demand matching strategies, monitor supply status in real-time, and provide detailed early warning information and decision support, significantly improving the stability and response capability of product supply services.
