SCER Management AI案例研究
SCER Management AI Case Study

AI驱动的供应链事件响应管理优化解决方案，通过约束满足+时序预测+强化学习排程技术提高计划达成率，降低缺货率，并提升物料准时交付率 AI-driven supply chain event response management optimization solutions improve schedule adherence, reduce stock-out rates, and enhance material on-time delivery through constraint satisfaction + time series forecasting + reinforcement learning scheduling technology

AI应用场景
AI Application Scenarios

约束满足
Constraint Satisfaction

利用约束满足算法处理供应链中的多重约束条件，包括资源限制、时间窗口和优先级规则，实现最优的计划排程和资源分配，提高计划可行性和执行效率。 Using constraint satisfaction algorithms to handle multiple constraints in supply chain, including resource limitations, time windows and priority rules, achieving optimal schedule planning and resource allocation, improving plan feasibility and execution efficiency.

时序预测（ETA/良率）
Time Series Forecasting (ETA/Yield Rate)

通过时间序列预测模型，准确预测事件响应时间（ETA）和过程良率，实现提前预警和主动调整，提高供应链响应速度和产品质量。 Using time series forecasting models to accurately predict event response time (ETA) and process yield rate, achieving early warning and proactive adjustment, improving supply chain response speed and product quality.

启发式/强化学习排程
Heuristic/Reinforcement Learning Scheduling

结合启发式算法和强化学习技术，动态优化排程策略，自适应调整计划执行路径，最大化计划达成率和资源效率。 Combining heuristic algorithms and reinforcement learning technology to dynamically optimize scheduling strategies, adaptively adjust plan execution paths, maximizing schedule adherence and resource efficiency.

戴尔科技作为全球领先的科技解决方案提供商，通过实施AI驱动的SCER管理系统，实现了供应链事件响应的智能化管理。该系统基于约束满足、时序预测和强化学习排程技术，为全球供应链网络提供全面的计划优化和事件响应解决方案。 As a global leading technology solutions provider, Dell Technologies implemented an AI-driven SCER management system, achieving intelligent management of supply chain event response. The system is based on constraint satisfaction, time series forecasting and reinforcement learning scheduling technologies, providing comprehensive plan optimization and event response solutions for global supply chain networks.

通过实施AI驱动的SCER管理系统，戴尔成功将计划达成率提高了12个百分点，承诺兑现率提高了13个百分点，缺货率降低了25%，加急成本降低了18%，物料准时交付率提高了8个百分点。系统能够智能处理供应链多重约束，准确预测事件响应时间，动态优化排程策略，并提供详细的事件响应建议和风险评估，大幅提高了供应链的稳定性和响应能力。 By implementing the AI-driven SCER management system, Dell successfully increased schedule adherence by 12 percentage points, commitment fulfillment rate by 13 percentage points, reduced stock-out rate by 25%, lowered expedited costs by 18%, and improved material on-time delivery by 8 percentage points. The system can intelligently handle multiple supply chain constraints, accurately predict event response time, dynamically optimize scheduling strategies, and provide detailed event response suggestions and risk assessments, significantly improving supply chain stability and response capability.
