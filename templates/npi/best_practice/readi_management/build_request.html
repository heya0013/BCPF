BOM/文件知识图谱
BOM/Document Knowledge Graph

利用知识图谱技术构建BOM（物料清单）和工程文档的语义关系网络，实现跨文档的智能关联和检索， 提高Build Request审查的准确性和完整性。 Using knowledge graph technology to build semantic relationship networks for BOM (Bill of Materials) and engineering documents, achieving intelligent cross-document association and retrieval, improving the accuracy and completeness of Build Request review.

  • BOM关联准确率提升至95%
    BOM association accuracy increased to 95%
  • 文档检索效率提高60-75%
    Document retrieval efficiency improved by 60-75%
  • 审查覆盖率提高30-40%
    Review coverage improved by 30-40%

影响范围推理
Impact Scope Reasoning

通过图神经网络和推理算法，自动分析工程变更的影响范围和依赖关系， 识别潜在风险点，提供全面的影响评估和优化建议。 Using graph neural networks and reasoning algorithms to automatically analyze the impact scope and dependencies of engineering changes, identifying potential risk points, providing comprehensive impact assessment and optimization suggestions.

  • 影响分析准确率提高至92%
    Impact analysis accuracy increased to 92%
  • 风险评估时间缩短50-65%
    Risk assessment time shortened by 50-65%
  • 变更冲突识别率提升80%
    Change conflict identification rate improved by 80%

版本一致性校验
Version Consistency Verification

利用AI算法自动检测BOM版本、设计文档和代码库之间的一致性， 确保Build Request的完整性和可执行性，减少版本冲突和集成问题。 Using AI algorithms to automatically detect consistency between BOM versions, design documents and code repositories, ensuring the integrity and executability of Build Requests, reducing version conflicts and integration issues.

  • 一致性校验准确率提高至90%
    Consistency verification accuracy increased to 90%
  • 版本冲突减少40-55%
    Version conflicts reduced by 40-55%
  • 集成问题识别率提升70%
    Integration issue identification rate improved by 70%

西门子作为全球领先的工业自动化和数字化解决方案提供商，通过在其Teamcenter PLM系统中集成AI驱动的Build Request Review功能，实现了工程变更请求的智能化审查和管理。该系统基于BOM/文件知识图谱、影响范围推理和版本一致性校验技术，为全球制造企业提供全面的变更管理解决方案。 As a global leader in industrial automation and digitalization solutions, Siemens integrated AI-driven Build Request Review functionality into its Teamcenter PLM system, achieving intelligent review and management of engineering change requests. The system is based on BOM/document knowledge graph, impact scope reasoning and version consistency verification technologies, providing comprehensive change management solutions for global manufacturing enterprises.

通过实施AI驱动的Build Request Review系统，西门子成功将ECR处理周期缩短了28%，同步遗漏率降低了55%，上线缺陷率降低了45%，审查效率提升了42%。系统能够自动构建BOM知识图谱，智能分析变更影响范围，实时校验版本一致性，并提供详细的变更建议和风险评估，大幅提高了工程变更的效率和质量。 By implementing the AI-driven Build Request Review system, Siemens successfully reduced ECR processing cycle by 28%, synchronization omission rate by 55%, launch defect rate by 45%, and improved review efficiency by 42%. The system can automatically build BOM knowledge graphs, intelligently analyze change impact scope, verify version consistency in real-time, and provide detailed change suggestions and risk assessments, significantly improving the efficiency and quality of engineering changes.
