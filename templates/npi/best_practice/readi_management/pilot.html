赛米控（SEMIKRON） - 功率模块Pilot&Ramp&Launch管理中的仿真应用
SEMIKRON - Simulation Application in Power Module Pilot&Ramp&Launch Management

赛米控国际在其功率模块产品的Pilot&Ramp&Launch管理过程中，采用基于SEMI/E仿真方法的SEMISEL平台， 实现了产品开发流程的数字化仿真和优化。该平台通过离散事件仿真技术，模拟产品从设计到量产的完整流程， 显著提升了产品上市效率和质量。 SEMIKRON International adopted the SEMISEL platform based on SEMI/E simulation methods in its power module product Pilot&Ramp&Launch management process, achieving digital simulation and optimization of the product development process. The platform simulates the complete process from design to mass production through discrete event simulation technology, significantly improving product launch efficiency and quality.

通过实施AI驱动的时序预测和离散事件仿真，赛米控成功将R@R（Ready-to-Ramp）通过率提升了12个百分点， Ramp延误率降低了22%，首月DPPM（Defective Parts Per Million）降低了18%。系统能够精准预测 产品上市过程中的关键里程碑，优化资源分配，并通过策略推荐引擎提供最优上市方案。 By implementing AI-driven time series forecasting and discrete event simulation, SEMIKRON successfully increased R@R (Ready-to-Ramp) pass rate by 12 percentage points, reduced Ramp delay rate by 22%, and reduced first-month DPPM (Defective Parts Per Million) by 18%. The system can accurately predict key milestones in the product launch process, optimize resource allocation, and provide optimal launch solutions through a strategy recommendation engine.

关键技术： 离散事件仿真、时序预测算法、策略推荐引擎

Key Technologies: Discrete event simulation, time series forecasting algorithms, strategy recommendation engine
实施范围： 全球功率模块产品线，涵盖IGBT、SiC MOSFET等关键器件
Implementation Scope: Global power module product lines, covering key devices such as IGBT and SiC MOSFET
效果持续时间： 持续优化超过3年
Effect Duration: Continuous optimization for over 3 years

案例出处: 赛米控SEMISEL仿真平台 - 功率模块设计仿真最佳实践
