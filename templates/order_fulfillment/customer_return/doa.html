Order DOA AI Case Study

AI驱动的到货即损订单处理优化解决方案，通过政策匹配+金额核算+凭证校验+机器人流程自动化（RPA）技术提高退款时效，优化补发OTIF，提升客户满意度，并降低运营成本 AI-driven Dead on Arrival order processing optimization solutions improve refund timeliness, optimize replacement OTIF, enhance customer satisfaction, and reduce operational costs through policy matching + amount accounting + voucher verification + robotic process automation (RPA) technology

AI应用场景
AI Application Scenarios

政策匹配
Policy Matching

利用自然语言处理和知识图谱技术自动匹配客户退货政策与订单具体情况，基于历史数据和合同条款，精准识别适用的退货政策和补偿标准，确保处理准确性和合规性。 Using natural language processing and knowledge graph technology to automatically match customer return policies with specific order situations, based on historical data and contract terms, accurately identifying applicable return policies and compensation standards, ensuring processing accuracy and compliance.

金额核算
Amount Accounting

通过智能算法自动核算退款或补偿金额，考虑产品价值、使用情况、折旧率和保险条款，生成准确的金额计算方案，减少人工计算错误和争议。 Automatically calculating refund or compensation amounts through intelligent algorithms, considering product value, usage, depreciation rate and insurance terms, generating accurate amount calculation solutions, reducing manual calculation errors and disputes.

凭证校验
Voucher Verification

基于计算机视觉和规则引擎自动验证退货凭证的真实性和完整性，包括发票、检测报告和照片证据，提高验证准确性和效率，减少欺诈风险。 Automatically verifying the authenticity and integrity of return vouchers based on computer vision and rule engines, including invoices, inspection reports and photo evidence, improving verification accuracy and efficiency, reducing fraud risk.

机器人流程自动化（RPA）
Robotic Process Automation (RPA)

通过RPA技术自动化执行重复性高的订单处理任务，包括数据录入、系统更新和通知发送，提高处理效率，减少人工错误，释放人力资源。 Automating highly repetitive order processing tasks through RPA technology, including data entry, system updates and notification sending, improving processing efficiency, reducing manual errors, and freeing up human resources.
