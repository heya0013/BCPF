AI Application Scenarios

集成质量监测
Integrated Quality Monitoring

利用计算机视觉和传感器技术实时监测分拣过程中的质量指标，包括物品完整性、标签准确性和包装合规性，确保分拣质量符合标准，减少异常漏读和丢码情况。 Using computer vision and sensor technology to monitor quality indicators in the sorting process in real time, including item integrity, label accuracy and packaging compliance, ensuring sorting quality meets standards, reducing abnormal missed reads and lost codes.

字段一致性校验
Field Consistency Verification

通过智能校验算法自动验证分拣数据字段的一致性和准确性，包括订单信息、产品规格和目的地数据，识别不一致和错误字段，提高数据质量和分拣准确性。 Using intelligent verification algorithms to automatically verify the consistency and accuracy of sorting data fields, including order information, product specifications and destination data, identifying inconsistent and erroneous fields, improving data quality and sorting accuracy.

落地效果回溯
Implementation Effect Backtracking

基于数据分析和回溯技术，自动追踪分拣履行的实际效果，包括分拣准确性、时效性和客户满意度，识别改进机会和优化点，提高分拣流程的持续改进能力。 Based on data analysis and backtracking technology, automatically tracking the actual effects of sorting fulfillment, including sorting accuracy, timeliness and customer satisfaction, identifying improvement opportunities and optimization points, improving the continuous improvement capability of the sorting process.

山东汉鑫科技股份有限公司专注于人工智能技术智能化应用，开发了基于AI的智能刹车盘识别分拣系统。该系统应用于全球知名刹车盘生产企业，解决了传统分拣流程中依赖人工干预、工序复杂、成本高昂的问题。 Shandong Hanxin Technology Co., Ltd. focuses on intelligent applications of artificial intelligence technology, developing an AI-based intelligent brake disc recognition and sorting system. The system is applied to a globally renowned brake disc manufacturer, solving the problems of reliance on manual intervention, complex processes and high costs in traditional sorting processes.

传统分拣流程需要多道工序和人工干预：在分类的前一环节为刹车盘铸造特定的型号编码钢印，并配备工人将刹车盘翻面确保钢印朝上，以便工业相机识别编码，分类后还需用专用刀具将钢印打磨擦除。这不仅增加了企业成本，还影响了产品质量。 The traditional sorting process required multiple processes and manual intervention: casting specific model code stamps on brake discs in the previous step of classification, assigning workers to flip the brake discs to ensure the stamps face up for industrial camera recognition, and then using special tools to grind and erase the stamps after classification. This not only increased enterprise costs but also affected product quality.

通过实施汉鑫科技的AI智能分拣系统，企业实现了刹车盘分拣的全面自动化。系统基于华为云ModelArts人工智能开发平台，自动挖掘和提取不同刹车盘之间的形状、大小、轮廓、纹理等特征差异，建立不同型号刹车盘的特征空间，实现精准分类。系统采用边云协同部署方式，通过华为云Hilens平台将云上算法下发至边缘计算服务器，满足车间离线使用需求，单个图像识别在500ms内完成。 By implementing Hanxin Technology's AI intelligent sorting system, the enterprise achieved comprehensive automation of brake disc sorting. The system is based on Huawei Cloud ModelArts artificial intelligence development platform, automatically mining and extracting feature differences such as shape, size, contour, and texture between different brake discs, establishing feature spaces for different models of brake discs, achieving accurate classification. The system adopts an edge-cloud collaborative deployment method, delivering cloud algorithms to edge computing servers through Huawei Cloud Hilens platform, meeting the offline usage needs of the workshop, with single image recognition completed within 500ms.

该系统还提供智能分拣管理系统，具备实时图片识别展示、分拣追溯、统计报表等功能。企业能够实时获取图像并显示，进行直观的检测分析；针对识别准确率低的图片进行追溯、归档，并上传至ModelArts平台优化算法模型，用于新型号的训练及原有型号识别准确率的提高。 The system also provides an intelligent sorting management system with functions such as real-time image recognition display, sorting traceability, and statistical reports. Enterprises can obtain and display images in real time for intuitive detection and analysis; trace and archive images with low recognition accuracy, and upload them to the ModelArts platform to optimize algorithm models for new model training and improvement of recognition accuracy of existing models.

实施AI解决方案后，该刹车盘生产企业实现了识别准确度达到99.99%，分拣效率提升10%，人工干预减少60%。系统直接消除了人工翻面、铸造钢印、擦除钢印等多道工序，显著提高了产品质量和生产效率，真正做到了为企业提质、降本、增效。 After implementing the AI solution, the brake disc manufacturer achieved a recognition accuracy of 99.99%, a 10% improvement in sorting efficiency, and a 60% reduction in manual intervention. The system directly eliminated multiple processes such as manual flipping, stamp casting, and stamp erasing, significantly improving product quality and production efficiency, truly achieving quality improvement, cost reduction, and efficiency enhancement for the enterprise.

汉鑫科技的AI智能分拣系统特别注重分拣质量的集成监测和分拣效果的持续优化。通过AI技术，系统实现了刹车盘分拣全流程的智能化管理，包括质量监测、数据校验和效果回溯。该系统不仅应用于刹车盘分拣，还可推广至金属冶炼、服装、制造等多个行业的智能分拣场景，为企业数字化转型升级提供了新路径。 Hanxin Technology's AI intelligent sorting system particularly focuses on integrated monitoring of sorting quality and continuous optimization of sorting effects. Through AI technology, the system achieves intelligent management of the entire brake disc sorting process, including quality monitoring, data verification, and effect backtracking. The system is not only applied to brake disc sorting but can also be extended to intelligent sorting scenarios in various industries such as metal smelting, clothing, and manufacturing, providing new paths for enterprise digital transformation and upgrading.
