AI Application Scenarios

截单时间约束优化
Cut-off Time Constraint Optimization

利用优化算法自动处理截单时间约束，基于订单处理能力、运输时间和客户要求，智能调整截单策略，确保订单按时处理并最大化截单窗口利用率。 Using optimization algorithms to automatically handle cut-off time constraints, intelligently adjusting cut-off strategies based on order processing capacity, transportation time and customer requirements, ensuring timely order processing and maximizing cut-off window utilization.

承运商预约与门位容量优化
Carrier Appointment and Door Capacity Optimization

通过智能调度算法优化承运商预约和门位容量分配，考虑门位可用性、装卸效率和设备资源，实现门位利用率和装卸效率的最大化。 Optimizing carrier appointments and door capacity allocation through intelligent scheduling algorithms, considering door availability, loading/unloading efficiency and equipment resources, achieving maximum door utilization and handling efficiency.

优先级权重联合优化
Priority Weighting Joint Optimization

基于多目标优化技术，将订单优先级作为权重因子，联合优化分组策略和门位计划，确保高优先级订单获得最优处理，同时平衡整体运营效率。 Based on multi-objective optimization technology, using order priority as weighting factors, jointly optimizing grouping strategies and door plans, ensuring optimal processing for high-priority orders while balancing overall operational efficiency.
