Order Reroute AI Case Study

AI应用场景
AI Application Scenarios

异常分类与优先级再评分
Exception Classification and Priority Re-scoring

利用机器学习算法自动识别和分类订单异常类型，包括短拣、缺货、质量问题等，基于服务等级和截单时间对异常订单进行优先级再评分，确保高优先级订单获得优先处理。 Using machine learning algorithms to automatically identify and classify order exception types, including short-picks, stockouts, quality issues, etc., re-scoring exception order priority based on service level and cut-off time, ensuring high-priority orders receive priority processing.

重分组仿真
Regrouping Simulation

通过数字孪生技术构建订单重分组仿真环境，模拟不同重分组策略对订单处理效率和资源利用率的影响，评估各种场景下的重分组效果，优化重分组决策的准确性和可靠性。 Building order regrouping simulation environments through digital twin technology, simulating the impact of different regrouping strategies on order processing efficiency and resource utilization, evaluating regrouping effects in various scenarios, optimizing the accuracy and reliability of regrouping decisions.

承运商改配推荐
Carrier Reassignment Recommendation

基于智能推荐算法，自动识别和推荐最优的承运商改配方案，考虑承运商能力、成本效益和服务质量，确保订单重路由的效率和可靠性，提高客户满意度。 Based on intelligent recommendation algorithms, automatically identifying and recommending optimal carrier reassignment solutions, considering carrier capacity, cost-effectiveness and service quality, ensuring the efficiency and reliability of order rerouting, improving customer satisfaction.
