Dynamic Sourcing AI Case Study

AI驱动的动态寻源优化解决方案，通过缺货风险预测+预补触发+库位重排推荐技术降低短拣率，减少拣选中断次数，优化出库周期，并提升动态寻源效率和准确性 AI-driven dynamic sourcing optimization solutions reduce short-pick rate, decrease picking interruptions, optimize outbound cycle, and improve dynamic sourcing efficiency and accuracy through stockout risk prediction + pre-replenishment triggering + location rearrangement recommendation technology

AI应用场景
AI Application Scenarios

缺货风险预测
Stockout Risk Prediction

利用机器学习算法分析历史销售数据、季节性模式和实时库存水平，精准预测潜在的缺货风险，提前触发补货流程，确保库存充足性和订单履行率。 Using machine learning algorithms to analyze historical sales data, seasonal patterns and real-time inventory levels, accurately predicting potential stockout risks, triggering replenishment processes in advance, ensuring inventory adequacy and order fulfillment rate.

预补触发
Pre-replenishment Triggering

基于智能触发机制，自动识别需要预补货的商品和时机，考虑补货提前期、安全库存和需求波动，实现精准的自动补货触发，减少人工干预和缺货风险。 Based on intelligent triggering mechanisms, automatically identifying products and timing for pre-replenishment, considering replenishment lead time, safety stock and demand fluctuations, achieving accurate automatic replenishment triggering, reducing manual intervention and stockout risks.

库位重排推荐
Location Rearrangement Recommendation

通过优化算法分析商品关联性、拣选频率和存储特性，智能推荐最优的库位布局和重排策略，提高拣选效率和仓库空间利用率，降低拣选错误率。 Using optimization algorithms to analyze product correlations, picking frequency and storage characteristics, intelligently recommending optimal location layouts and rearrangement strategies, improving picking efficiency and warehouse space utilization, reducing picking error rates.
