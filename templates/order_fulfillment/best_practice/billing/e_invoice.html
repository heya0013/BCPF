AI应用场景
AI Application Scenarios

影像/签名识别
Image/Signature Recognition

利用计算机视觉和深度学习技术自动识别发票图像中的关键信息和签名区域，通过OCR技术提取发票数据，验证签名真实性，确保发票信息的完整性和准确性。 Using computer vision and deep learning technology to automatically identify key information and signature areas in invoice images, extracting invoice data through OCR technology, verifying signature authenticity, ensuring the integrity and accuracy of invoice information.

里程碑一致性校验
Milestone Consistency Verification

基于智能校验算法自动验证业务里程碑与发票信息的一致性，包括订单日期、交付时间和服务完成状态，确保开票时机和内容的准确性，减少差错和争议。 Based on intelligent verification algorithms, automatically verifying the consistency between business milestones and invoice information, including order date, delivery time and service completion status, ensuring the accuracy of billing timing and content, reducing errors and disputes.

收入确认规则引擎
Revenue Recognition Rule Engine

通过智能规则引擎自动化执行收入确认流程，基于会计准则和公司政策，自动匹配收入与相关成本，生成准确的收入确认记录，提高财务报告的可靠性。 Automating revenue recognition processes through intelligent rule engines, based on accounting standards and company policies, automatically matching revenue with related costs, generating accurate revenue recognition records, improving the reliability of financial reporting.
