制造端入厂 In-bound to MFG

负责将生产所需的原材料、零部件等从供应商运输至工厂，保障生产物料按时到位。 Be responsible for transporting raw materials, components, etc. needed for production from suppliers to the factory to ensure that production materials arrive on time.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

货物运输里程碑与状态 Shipment Milestone And Status
核心价值 Core Value

运输计划/承运商选择/费率管理决定时效与成本，是端到端履约的主干流程；仓内入库/拣选/波次/装载与码头排程直接影响出入库效率与发运达成。 Transport planning/carrier selection/rate management determine timeliness and cost, which is the backbone process of end-to-end fulfillment; warehouse inbound/picking/wave/loading and dock scheduling directly affect inbound and outbound efficiency and shipping achievement.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 统一 OTD/OTIF、里程碑、异常与成本口径，构建 lane / 承运商 / 客户维度的可视化 Unify the standards for OTD/OTIF, milestones, abnormalities, and costs, and build visualization by lane/carrier/customer dimensions.
  • 基于异常根因 / 严重度自动编排 SLA 与补救动作 Automatically arrange SLAs (Service Level Agreements) and remedial actions based on the root cause/severity of abnormalities.
