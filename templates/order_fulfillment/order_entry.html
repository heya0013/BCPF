订单录入 In-bound to MFG

接收并录入客户订单信息的初始环节。 The initial stage of receiving and entering customer order information.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

信用管理 Credit Management
核心价值 Core Value

信用与条款：授信额度、账期与逾期状态为硬性前置，防止无效承诺与拒收。；下发放行：向仓储系统下发并锁定库存，将优先级信息一并传递。 Credit and Terms: Credit limits, payment terms, and overdue status are hard prerequisites to prevent invalid commitments and order rejection. ; Issue Release Instructions: Send release instructions to the warehouse management system, lock the corresponding inventory, and transmit priority information simultaneously.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 接入客户授信、账期与逾期记录，对异常订单自动挂起并回写原因，减少后段拒收 Integrate customer credit, payment terms, and overdue records to automatically suspend abnormal orders and write back reasons, reducing downstream order rejection.

信用放行 Credit Release
核心价值 Core Value

异常拦截：禁运、超额、信息缺失等进入挂起，等待处理后再放行。；下发放行：向仓储系统下发并锁定库存，将优先级信息一并传递。 Exception Interception: Issues such as embargoes, exceeding limits, and missing information will be put on hold, awaiting processing before release. ; Issue Release Instructions: Send release instructions to the warehouse management system, lock the corresponding inventory, and transmit priority information simultaneously.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 用大模型从客户 PO / 邮件 / 门户自动抽取字段，匹配主数据并提示缺失项，异常自动拦截与回询 Use LLMs to automatically extract fields from customer POs/emails/portals, match them with master data, and prompt for missing items. Automatically intercept and inquire about anomalies.

Order Release
核心价值

信用与条款：授信额度、账期与逾期状态为硬性前置，防止无效承诺与拒收。；下发放行：向仓储系统下发并锁定库存，将优先级信息一并传递。 Credit and Terms: Credit limits, payment terms, and overdue status are hard prerequisites to prevent invalid commitments and refusals. ; Release Instructions: Send release instructions to the warehouse management system, lock the corresponding inventory, and transmit priority information simultaneously.

优化建议：
  • 结合在库、在制、在途与产能计算可承诺时间窗；缺货时给出替代料或分批承诺方案并对客户展示可解释理由
    • Combine inventory, work-in-progress, in-transit, and capacity data to calculate a reliable promise window; in case of stockouts, provide alternative materials or partial fulfillment plans with explainable reasons for customers.
