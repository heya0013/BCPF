EMS Replenishment Planning AI Case Study

AI驱动的电子制造服务补货规划优化解决方案，通过条码/RFID比对+异常分类+重拣/返工策略推荐技术降低差错率，缩短异常处理时长，提升OTIF，并提高补货规划效率和准确性 AI-driven electronic manufacturing service replenishment planning optimization solutions reduce error rates, shorten exception handling time, improve OTIF, and enhance replenishment planning efficiency and accuracy through barcode/RFID comparison + exception classification + re-pick/rework strategy recommendation technology

AI应用场景
AI Application Scenarios

条码/RFID比对
Barcode/RFID Comparison

利用计算机视觉和RFID技术自动识别和比对物料条码信息，实时验证物料与订单的一致性，减少人工比对错误，提高物料识别的准确性和效率。 Using computer vision and RFID technology to automatically identify and compare material barcode information, verifying the consistency between materials and orders in real time, reducing manual comparison errors, improving the accuracy and efficiency of material identification.

异常分类
Exception Classification

通过机器学习算法自动识别和分类补货流程中的异常情况，包括物料不匹配、数量差异和质量问题，实现异常情况的精准识别和快速响应。 Using machine learning algorithms to automatically identify and classify exceptions in the replenishment process, including material mismatches, quantity differences and quality issues, achieving accurate identification and rapid response to exceptions.

重拣/返工策略推荐
Re-pick/Rework Strategy Recommendation

基于智能推荐算法，自动生成最优的重拣或返工策略，考虑物料可用性、产线需求和时效要求，确保异常情况得到快速有效的处理，最小化对生产计划的影响。 Based on intelligent recommendation algorithms, automatically generating optimal re-pick or rework strategies, considering material availability, production line requirements and timeliness requirements, ensuring rapid and effective handling of exceptions, minimizing the impact on production schedules.
