AI驱动的库存预留优化解决方案，通过路径优化+拥堵预测+任务分配与调度技术提高拣选效率，优化行走距离，提升人机协同效率，降低单位履约成本，并提升库存预留管理效率和资源利用率 AI-driven inventory reservation optimization solutions improve picking efficiency, optimize walking distance, enhance human-machine collaboration efficiency, reduce unit fulfillment cost, and improve inventory reservation management efficiency and resource utilization through path optimization + congestion prediction + task allocation and scheduling technology

AI应用场景
AI Application Scenarios

路径优化
Path Optimization

利用智能算法分析仓库布局和订单特征，自动规划最优拣选路径，考虑货位距离、拣选顺序和行走效率，减少无效行走距离，提高拣选效率。 Using intelligent algorithms to analyze warehouse layout and order characteristics, automatically planning optimal picking paths, considering slot distance, picking sequence and walking efficiency, reducing ineffective walking distance, improving picking efficiency.

拥堵预测
Congestion Prediction

通过机器学习算法预测仓库内可能出现的拥堵区域和时段，基于历史数据和实时状态，提前进行资源调配和路径调整，避免生产中断和效率下降。 Using machine learning algorithms to predict potential congestion areas and periods in the warehouse, based on historical data and real-time status, pre-allocating resources and adjusting paths to avoid production interruptions and efficiency degradation.

任务分配与调度
Task Allocation and Scheduling

基于智能调度算法优化任务分配和资源调度，考虑员工技能、工作负荷和设备状态，实现人机协同效率最大化，提高整体作业效率。 Optimizing task allocation and resource scheduling based on intelligent scheduling algorithms, considering employee skills, workload and equipment status, achieving maximum human-machine collaboration efficiency, improving overall operational efficiency.
