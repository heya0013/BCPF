计费与支付 Billing & Payment

处理订单的发票开具和付款流程。 Handles the invoicing and payment processing for orders.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

订单电子发票 Order E-invoice
核心价值 Core Value

开票合规与税控：税率/税码/抬头/明细与履约一致；电子发票/税控平台直连减少退票。 Invoicing Compliance and Tax Control: Tax rates/codes/titles/details are consistent with fulfillment; direct connection to electronic invoicing/tax control platforms reduces invoice returns.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 以电子签收影像 / 坐标作为计费 / 开票 / 收入确认的证据前置；按 IFRS 15 履约义务进行确认。 Use electronic signature images/coordinates as evidence for billing/invoicing/revenue recognition; confirm according to IFRS 15 performance obligations.

客户账单流程 Customer Billing
核心价值 Core Value

开票合规与税控：税率 / 税码 / 抬头 / 明细与履约一致；电子发票 / 税控平台直连减少退票。 Invoicing Compliance and Tax Control: Tax rates/codes/titles/details are consistent with fulfillment; direct connection to electronic invoicing/tax control platforms reduces invoice returns.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 按合同费率 / 附加费与运单事件自动计费；治理费率版本与费目映射，减少错计漏计。 Automatically bill according to contract rates/additional fees and waybill events; govern rate versions and fee item mapping to reduce miscalculations and omissions.
