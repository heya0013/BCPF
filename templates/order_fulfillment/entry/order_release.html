Order Release AI案例研究
Order Release AI Case Study

AI驱动的订单发布优化解决方案，通过交期预测+替代料推荐+分批承诺仿真技术提高如约足量准时交付率（OTIF），降低缺货率，提升承诺命中率，并优化订单管理效率 AI-driven order release optimization solutions improve on-time in-full delivery rate (OTIF), reduce stockout rate, enhance commitment hit rate, and optimize order management efficiency through delivery date prediction + substitute material recommendation + batch commitment simulation technology

AI应用场景
AI Application Scenarios

交期预测
Delivery Date Prediction

利用机器学习算法分析历史订单数据、供应链状况和生产能力，精准预测订单交付日期，考虑多种动态因素如供应商绩效、物流延迟和产能波动，提高交付时间准确性。 Using machine learning algorithms to analyze historical order data, supply chain conditions and production capacity, accurately predicting order delivery dates, considering multiple dynamic factors such as supplier performance, logistics delays and capacity fluctuations, improving delivery time accuracy.

替代料推荐
Substitute Material Recommendation

通过智能推荐系统，在原材料短缺或成本过高时自动识别和推荐最合适的替代材料，基于技术规格、成本效益和供应可用性进行优化选择，确保生产连续性。 Through an intelligent recommendation system, automatically identifying and recommending the most suitable substitute materials when raw materials are in short supply or too expensive, optimizing selection based on technical specifications, cost-effectiveness and supply availability, ensuring production continuity.

分批承诺仿真
Batch Commitment Simulation

基于仿真技术，模拟不同分批承诺策略对供应链的影响，评估各种场景下的交付风险和资源利用率，优化订单发布决策，提高承诺命中率和客户满意度。 Based on simulation technology, simulating the impact of different batch commitment strategies on the supply chain, evaluating delivery risks and resource utilization in various scenarios, optimizing order release decisions, improving commitment hit rate and customer satisfaction.

微软Azure OpenAI与SAP S/4HANA的集成为Order Release流程带来了革命性的改进。通过AI技术，企业能够自动化处理销售订单创建、优化交付日期预测，并实现智能替代料推荐，显著提升了订单发布效率和准确性。 The integration of Microsoft Azure OpenAI with SAP S/4HANA has brought revolutionary improvements to the Order Release process. Through AI technology, enterprises can automate sales order creation, optimize delivery date prediction, and achieve intelligent substitute material recommendation, significantly improving order release efficiency and accuracy.

在这一解决方案中，Azure OpenAI的自然语言处理能力被用于自动从客户邮件中提取订单信息，并在SAP S/4HANA中自动创建销售订单。系统能够理解邮件中的非结构化内容，准确识别产品详情、数量要求和交付期望，大大减少了人工数据输入的需要。 In this solution, Azure OpenAI's natural language processing capability is used to automatically extract order information from customer emails and automatically create sales orders in SAP S/4HANA. The system can understand unstructured content in emails, accurately identify product details, quantity requirements and delivery expectations, greatly reducing the need for manual data entry.

此外，AI驱动的交期预测系统通过分析历史交付数据、当前产能状况和供应链风险因素，提供更准确的交付日期承诺。替代料推荐引擎则在原材料短缺时，基于技术兼容性、成本影响和供应可用性，智能推荐最佳替代方案，确保生产计划不受影响。 Additionally, the AI-driven delivery date prediction system provides more accurate delivery date commitments by analyzing historical delivery data, current capacity status and supply chain risk factors. The substitute material recommendation engine intelligently recommends the best alternatives based on technical compatibility, cost impact and supply availability when raw materials are in short supply, ensuring production plans are not affected.

分批承诺仿真功能允许企业在发布订单前模拟不同策略的影响，评估各种场景下的资源利用率和交付风险，从而做出更明智的决策。这一AI驱动的解决方案使企业能够实现OTIF提升3.2个百分点，缺货率降低22%，承诺命中率提高6.5个百分点，订单处理时间减少40%。 The batch commitment simulation function allows enterprises to simulate the impact of different strategies before releasing orders, evaluating resource utilization and delivery risks in various scenarios, thus making more informed decisions. This AI-driven solution enables enterprises to achieve a 3.2 percentage point improvement in OTIF, a 22% reduction in stockout rate, a 6.5 percentage point increase in commitment hit rate, and a 40% reduction in order processing time.
