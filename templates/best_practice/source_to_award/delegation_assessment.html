规则引擎

Rule Engine

基于AI的智能规则引擎自动执行授权策略和审批流程，根据风险等级动态调整审批权限 AI-based intelligent rule engine automatically executes authorization policies and approval processes, dynamically adjusting approval permissions based on risk levels

  • 规则执行准确率提升至95% Rule execution accuracy increased to 95%
  • 审批流程自动化率提升70-80% Approval process automation rate increased by 70-80%

异常检测

Anomaly Detection

利用机器学习算法检测授权和审批过程中的异常模式，识别潜在合规风险和权限滥用 Using machine learning algorithms to detect abnormal patterns in authorization and approval processes, identifying potential compliance risks and permission abuse

  • 异常检测准确率提升至92% Anomaly detection accuracy increased to 92%
  • 风险识别时间缩短60-75% Risk identification time reduced by 60-75%

LLM报告生成

LLM Report Generation

利用大语言模型自动生成内部自评报告和审计摘要，提高报告质量和生成效率 Using large language models to automatically generate internal self-assessment reports and audit summaries, improving report quality and generation efficiency

  • 报告生成时间缩短75-85% Report generation time reduced by 75-85%
  • 报告质量提升40-50% Report quality improved by 40-50%
