可售库存

ATS (Available To Sell)
可售库存ATS指企业当前能直接卖给客户的商品数量。其核心价值在于通过动态管理库存（总库存减去已订单锁定等不可售部分），确保销售渠道显示真实库存，从而有效避免超卖缺货，提升订单履约能力。 Available To Sell (ATS) refers to the quantity of goods that an enterprise can directly sell to customers currently. Its core value lies in dynamically managing inventory (total inventory minus unsellable parts such as order-locked inventory) to ensure that sales channels display real inventory, thereby effectively avoiding overselling and stockouts and improving order fulfillment capabilities.

CV Itemgroup ATS
关键理由 Key Reasons

统一暴露可售可得量，将库存、在途、锁库与分配状态汇合，避免重复承诺，支撑跨渠道的实时可用性。 Unify the exposure of available sellable quantity, integrate inventory, in-transit, locked inventory, and allocation status to avoid duplicate commitments and support real-time availability across channels. ；Itemgroup/CV维度的ATS实现族群级聚合视角，便于按照产品系列或配置族进行策略分配与可视化。 ATS at the Itemgroup/CV dimension enables a group-level aggregated perspective, facilitating strategic allocation and visualization by product series or configuration families.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 建设ATS主数据与口径治理：统一库存状态枚举（在手/质检/在途/锁定/预约/分配）、暴露策略与时点规则 Establish ATS master data and caliber governance: unify inventory status enumeration (on-hand/quality inspection/in-transit/locked/reserved/allocated), exposure strategies, and timing rules
  • 基于对时点库存与在途的短期预测（短缺风险、到货偏差）+ 规则/优化策略，动态生成多时点ATS曲线 Dynamically generate multi-time-point ATS curves based on short-term forecasts of time-point inventory and in-transit (shortage risks, arrival deviations) + rules/optimization strategies
  • 提供‘Why/Why Not/Next Best’可售解释：为何此时不可售、下一个可售时点、需要哪些解锁动作（替代、转仓、替代料） Provide "Why/Why Not/Next Best" sellability explanations: why it is unsellable at this time, the next sellable time point, and what unlocking actions are needed (substitution, warehouse transfer, alternative materials)
