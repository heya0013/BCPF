工厂计划

Factory Planning
优化计划执行过程中的协作效率，确保各部门无缝衔接，提升整体计划效率。本流程涵盖物料追踪、订单承诺、发货通知等关键环节，帮助企业实现高效协同的计划执行。 Optimize collaboration efficiency during plan execution, ensure seamless connection between departments, and improve overall planning efficiency. This process covers key links such as material tracking, order promising, and shipment notification to help enterprises achieve efficient and collaborative plan execution.

工厂产能规划
Site Capacity Planning
核心价值 Core Value

该流程决定车间层的节奏与可执行性，对交付与成本具有决定性影响。 This process determines the rhythm and executability of the workshop level and has a decisive impact on delivery and costs.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 构建有限能力排程（APS/FCS）优化器，考虑瓶颈、换型、班次、物料齐套与优先级，多目标（OTS/吞吐/换型损失） Build a finite capacity scheduling (APS/FCS) optimizer that considers bottlenecks, model changes, shifts, material readiness and priorities, with multiple objectives (OTS/throughput/model change loss)
  • 建设车间数字孪生与达成看板，实时跟踪瓶颈、WIP与排程偏差，触发自动重排 Build a workshop digital twin and achievement dashboard to track bottlenecks, WIP and scheduling deviations in real time and trigger automatic rescheduling
