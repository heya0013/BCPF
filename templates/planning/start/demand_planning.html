需求计划

Demand Planning
基于历史数据与市场趋势进行需求预测，提升计划准确性，帮助企业实现生产、计划与销售的协同，降低不确定性与波动风险。 Conduct demand forecasting based on historical data and market trends to improve planning accuracy, help enterprises achieve collaboration between production, planning, and sales, and reduce uncertainty and fluctuation risks.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

长期预测
LTF (Long Term Forecast)
核心价值 Core Value

基线预测提供供给与库存策略的量化底座，需支持分层汇总一致性与偏差治理（MAPE/WAPE/Bias/FVA）。 Baseline forecasting provides a quantitative foundation for supply and inventory strategies, requiring support for hierarchical aggregation consistency and deviation management (MAPE/WAPE/Bias/FVA). "数据地基"，可形成对标与趋势预测，为RFx与合同价格变更提供定量依据。 As a "data foundation", it can form benchmarking and trend forecasting, providing quantitative basis for RFx and contract price changes.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 建立多模型融合的分层可解释预测（统计+ML+因果），结合Demand Sensing与促销/价格弹性建模 Establish hierarchical interpretable forecasting with multi-model integration (statistics + ML + causality), combined with Demand Sensing and promotion/price elasticity modeling
  • 以类比法+相似度检索+外部先行指标（搜索/媒体）构建NPI冷启动预测与替换链路管理 Construct NPI cold-start forecasting and replacement link management using analogy method + similarity retrieval + external leading indicators (search/media)
  • 严格执行FVA评估与偏差门禁，并将预测与S&OP/One Plan联动打通 Strictly implement FVA evaluation and deviation access control, and connect forecasting with S&OP/One Plan

细分产品长期预测
Segment Product LTF
核心价值 Core Value

该流程直接决定供需平衡的质量与稳定性，是端到端计划的起点。 This process directly determines the quality and stability of supply-demand balance and is the starting point of end-to-end planning. "路线图式"闭环，是季度/年度降本治理的主场，驱动跨职能协同落地。 As a "roadmap-style" closed loop, it is the main venue for quarterly/annual cost reduction management and drives the implementation of cross-functional collaboration.
