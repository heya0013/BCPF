物料需求计划

MRP
以需求计划为基础，分解产品结构，计算物料需求量与时间，确保原材料与零部件按时到位，支持生产计划顺利执行。 Based on demand planning, decompose product structure, calculate material demand quantity and timing, ensure raw materials and components arrive on time, and support the smooth execution of production plans.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

统一MRP与供应链网络集成
One MRP integration with Network
核心价值 Core Value

MRP负责在BOM层级进行净需求展开并生成计划建议，是计划与生产执行的主驱动，直接影响CTB与承诺稳定。 MRP is responsible for net demand expansion at the BOM level and generating planning suggestions, serving as the main driver of planning and production execution, directly affecting CTB and commitment stability. 供应商承诺/ASN与ETA预测是计划兑现的关键输入，决定MRP消息（加急/延后）的有效性。 Supplier commitments/ASN and ETA forecasts are key inputs for plan fulfillment, determining the effectiveness of MRP messages (urgent/delayed). MRP与ERP/MES集成（PO/WO下达、Reschedule、异常消息）是计划→执行闭环的核心，影响交付与成本。 Integration of MRP with ERP/MES (PO/WO release, Reschedule, exception messages) is the core of the planning→execution closed loop, affecting delivery and costs.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 基于供应商/通道/历史ASN构建ETA预测与承诺质量评分，驱动MRP消息阈值与优先级（加急/延后） Build ETA forecasts and commitment quality scores based on suppliers/channels/historical ASN to drive MRP message thresholds and priorities (urgent/delayed)
  • 用LLM对MRP异常消息智能分流（合并/去噪/指派），对低风险建议自动Firm/Release/Reschedule Use LLM for intelligent diversion of MRP exception messages (merge/denoise/assign), and automatically Firm/Release/Reschedule low-risk suggestions
  • 建立计划兑现率与FVA风格评估（MRP建议→实际交付），闭环优化参数（安全期、冻结窗、最小批量） Establish plan fulfillment rate and FVA style evaluation (MRP suggestions→actual delivery), and closed-loop optimize parameters (safety period, freeze window, minimum batch size)

Purchase Request (PR) Suggestion
核心价值 Core Value

MRP与ERP/MES集成（PO/WO下达、Reschedule、异常消息）是计划→执行闭环的核心，影响交付与成本。 Integration of MRP with ERP/MES (PO/WO release, Reschedule, exception messages) is the core of the planning→execution closed loop, affecting delivery and costs.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 对关键器件做滚动短缺预测，结合物料相似度/功能替代/兼容性规则生成替代推荐与审批流 Conduct rolling shortage forecasting for key components, generate alternative recommendations and approval flows based on material similarity/functional substitution/compatibility rules
  • 建立计划兑现率与FVA风格评估（MRP建议→实际交付），闭环优化参数（安全期、冻结窗、最小批量） Establish plan fulfillment rate and FVA style evaluation (MRP suggestions→actual delivery), and closed-loop optimize parameters (safety period, freeze window, minimum batch size)
