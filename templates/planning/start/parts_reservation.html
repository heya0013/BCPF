Parts Reservation
在库存或生产计划中提前锁定关键部件，保障优先订单与紧急需求的及时交付，降低生产延误与供应冲突风险。 Lock key parts in advance in inventory or production plans to ensure timely delivery of priority orders and urgent needs, and reduce the risks of production delays and supply conflicts.

Parts Reservation
核心价值 Core Value

Reservation/锁库直接改变可分配/可售池，是避免重复承诺与保障高优先级履约的关键执行抓手。 Reservation/warehouse locking directly changes the allocable/sellable pool, and is a key execution tool to avoid duplicate commitments and ensure high-priority fulfillment.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 建立‘软/硬锁+TTL’的策略引擎：按客户等级/渠道/促销/NPI设时效与额度，避免长期占用与虚占。 Establish a strategy engine of "soft/hard lock + TTL": set time limits and quotas according to customer level/channel/promotion/NPI to avoid long-term occupation and false occupation.
  • 用AI识别过度锁库/囤货/低转化锁库，自动提醒并触发回收审批。 Use AI to identify excessive warehouse locking/stockpiling/low-conversion warehouse locking, and automatically remind and trigger recovery approval.
  • 与ATS/ATP/DRP/MRP/MES实现事件驱动双向同步，提供‘Why/Why Not/Next Best’解释。 Realize event-driven two-way synchronization with ATS/ATP/DRP/MRP/MES, and provide "Why/Why Not/Next Best" explanations.
  • 建立锁库FVA/效应评估：锁库→承诺→履约→现金，量化正负效应并回调策略参数。 Establish warehouse locking FVA/effect evaluation: warehouse locking → commitment → fulfillment → cash, quantify positive and negative effects and adjust strategy parameters back.
