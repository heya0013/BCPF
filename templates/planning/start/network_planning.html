网络规划

Network Planning
其核心目标是构建一个高效、敏捷且成本最优的物流网络，以提升客户服务水平，同时平衡运输、仓储和库存持有总成本，增强供应链的整体韧性。 Its core goal is to build an efficient, agile, and cost-optimal logistics network to improve customer service levels, while balancing the total costs of transportation, warehousing, and inventory holding, and enhancing the overall resilience of the supply chain.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

ODM赋能
ODM Enablement
业务价值 Business Value

ODM/EMS Enablement 保证外协制造网络与自有网络口径一致，涵盖BOM/锁定/出货规则与在途对账，降低信息时延。 ODM/EMS Enablement ensures that the external manufacturing network is consistent with the internal network in terms of standards, covering BOM/locking/shipment rules and in-transit reconciliation, and reducing information latency.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 建立DRP/Deployment优化器，联合库存定位、运输时效与通道能力，按多目标（服务水平/总成本/现金占用）求解部署波次与路径。 Establish a DRP/Deployment optimizer that combines inventory positioning, transportation timeliness, and channel capacity, and solves deployment waves and paths based on multi-objectives (service level/total cost/cash occupation).
  • 建设在途与通道能力可视化（Lane Monitor），结合ETA预测，提前暴露拥堵与偏差并重算部署。 Build in-transit and channel capacity visualization (Lane Monitor), combine ETA forecasting to expose congestion and deviations in advance and recalculate deployment.
  • 固化网络定位与延迟配置策略（Postponement/Cross-dock），将S&OP政策落地为DRP规则。 Solidify network positioning and delay configuration strategies (Postponement/Cross-dock), and implement S&OP policies into DRP rules.
  • 推进 ODM/EMS Enablement：对齐BOM/锁定/出货窗口与在途对账，减少口径分裂与信息时延。 Promote ODM/EMS Enablement: align BOM/locking/shipment windows with in-transit reconciliation to reduce standard fragmentation and information latency.
