过渡计划

Transition Planning
在产品迭代或生产切换过程中，制定资源与产能的过渡方案，确保新旧产品平稳衔接，减少停工、浪费与供应链波动。 In the process of product iteration or production switching, formulate transition plans for resources and capacity to ensure smooth connection between old and new products, and reduce downtime, waste and supply chain fluctuations.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

零部件EC管理
Components EC Management

关键理由 Key Reason

Phase-in/Phase-out 与爬坡/降坡计划决定切换窗口与服务稳定，需与工厂/网络/分配协同。 Phase-in/Phase-out and ramp-up/ramp-down plans determine the switching window and service stability, and need to be coordinated with factories/networks/allocation.
