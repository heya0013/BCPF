负债管理

Liability Management
在产品迭代或生产切换过程中，制定资源与产能的过渡方案，确保新旧产品平稳衔接，减少停工、浪费与供应链波动。 During product iteration or production switching, formulate transition plans for resources and production capacity to ensure smooth connection between old and new products, and reduce downtime, waste, and supply chain fluctuations.

MWD端到端管理
MWD E2E Management
核心价值 Core Value

MWD/减值计提与冲回确保财务真实性，要求与库存/需求/转产策略联动，避免过计或漏计。 MWD/impairment provision and reversal ensure financial authenticity, requiring linkage with inventory/demand/production transfer strategies to avoid over-accrual or under-accrual.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 基于SKU×区域×通道建立责任金与E&O暴露预测，驱动S&OP红线与处置优先级。 Establish liability funds and E&O exposure forecasts based on SKU × region × channel to drive S&OP red lines and disposal priorities.
  • 对不同通道/区域做价格-销量响应建模与清仓路径优化，平衡回收率与速度。 Conduct price-sales response modeling and clearance path optimization for different channels/regions to balance recovery rate and speed.
  • 自动解析NCNR/MOQ/价保/回购条款，结合在途与需求偏差给出‘最小责任金’谈判建议。 Automatically parse NCNR/MOQ/price protection/buyback clauses, and provide "minimum liability fund" negotiation suggestions combined with in-transit and demand deviations.
  • 构建计划→财务桥接，自动计提/冲回MWD并给出差异解释，提升透明度与合规。 Build a planning→finance bridge, automatically accrue/reverse MWD and provide difference explanations to improve transparency and compliance.
