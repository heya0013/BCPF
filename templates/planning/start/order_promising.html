订单承诺

Order Promising
基于库存、产能与供应情况，快速判断订单能否按期交付，提升客户满意度并优化资源利用。 Based on inventory, production capacity and supply status, quickly determine whether orders can be delivered on time, improve customer satisfaction and optimize resource utilization.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

可建量（ATB）
Available to Build (ATB)
核心价值 Core Value

ATP/CTP/ATB 承诺引擎在物料、产能与运输约束下给出可行承诺日期（PSD/EDD），直接决定客户体验与改期率。 The ATP/CTP/ATB promising engine provides feasible commitment dates (PSD/EDD) under material, production capacity and transportation constraints, directly determining customer experience and rescheduling rate. 承诺需实时校验运输时效、产能排程与物料齐套（MRP/CTB/ETA），保障承诺可兑现。 Commitments need to real-time verify transportation timeliness, production capacity scheduling and material readiness (MRP/CTB/ETA) to ensure commitments can be fulfilled.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 构建多约束ATP/CTP引擎（材料、产能、运输、政策联合求解）。 Build a multi-constraint ATP/CTP engine (joint solution for materials, production capacity, transportation and policies).
  • 在缺料/拥堵时生成“下一最佳”方案（拆单、替代、跨库、改期+补偿），并量化客户体验与成本。 Generate "next best" solutions (order splitting, substitution, cross-warehouse, rescheduling + compensation) when materials are in short supply or there is congestion, and quantify customer experience and costs.
  • 建设全局ATP缓存（多站点/多渠道），CDC事件驱动的增量扣减与一致性校验，防止超卖。 Build a global ATP cache (multi-site/multi-channel), CDC event-driven incremental deduction and consistency verification to prevent overselling.
  • 基于ETA/产能/运输波动构建承诺风险评分，LLM对异常承诺分流处理，提前触发加急或替代 Build a commitment risk score based on ETA/production capacity/transportation fluctuations, and use LLM for diversion processing of abnormal commitments to trigger expedited or substitution in advance
