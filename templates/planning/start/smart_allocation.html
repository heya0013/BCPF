智能分配

Smart Allocation
通过融合规则、约束优化与机器学习的智能分配引擎，实现资源在供应、生产与需求端的高效匹配。该流程能够在多目标下（如服务水平、成本、利润）动态优化分配方案，提升计划灵活性和执行准确性。其核心环节包括分配策略设定、实时数据分析、自动决策与结果反馈，帮助企业在复杂环境中做出最优分配决策，平衡效率与韧性，构建敏捷、可持续的运营体系。 Through an intelligent allocation engine that integrates rules, constraint optimization, and machine learning, it achieves efficient matching of resources across supply, production, and demand sides. This process can dynamically optimize allocation plans under multiple objectives (such as service level, cost, profit), improving planning flexibility and execution accuracy. Its core links include allocation strategy setting, real-time data analysis, automatic decision-making, and result feedback, helping enterprises make optimal allocation decisions in complex environments, balance efficiency and resilience, and build an agile and sustainable operation system.

Smart Allocation

关键理由 Key Reason

将稀缺资源按客户/渠道/服务等级与价值优先级进行最优分配，直接影响高价值客户履约与OTIF；与ATS/ATP/CTB/DRP联动以减少改配并稳定首次承诺。 Optimal allocation of scarce resources according to customer/channel/service level and value priority directly affects the fulfillment and OTIF (On Time In Full) of high-value customers; linkage with ATS/ATP/CTB/DRP to reduce reallocation and stabilize initial commitments.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 构建可解释的分配优化器（多目标：服务等级/利润/违约成本/战略客户），支持策略参数与学习权重在线调优。 Build an interpretable allocation optimizer (multi-objective: service level/profit/default cost/strategic customers), supporting online tuning of strategy parameters and learning weights.
  • 建立‘策略参数中心’：客户分层/渠道优先级/合同SLA/利润贡献等主数据标准，贯通 ATS/ATP/CTB/DRP。 Establish a "strategy parameter center": master data standards such as customer stratification/channel priority/contract SLA/profit contribution, connecting ATS/ATP/CTB/DRP.
  • 对内外提供‘Why/Why Not/Next Best’的分配解释与替代建议，支撑销售与客户沟通。 Provide internal and external allocation explanations and alternative suggestions of "Why/Why Not/Next Best" to support sales and customer communication.
  • 将分配策略纳入 S&OP 门禁：异常阈值触发‘策略变更评审’，并做AB实验与回溯评估。 Incorporate allocation strategies into S&OP gatekeeping: abnormal thresholds trigger "strategy change review", and conduct AB tests and retrospective evaluations.
