本页展示长期预测在供应链规划中的应用场景、效果分析、关键KPI与行业落地案例，为长期规划与实施提供参考。 This page demonstrates the application scenarios, effect analysis, key KPIs, and industry implementation cases of long-term forecasting in supply chain planning, providing references for long-term planning and execution.

AI 应用场景 AI Application Scenarios

多模型集成与因果特征融合 Multi-Model Integration & Causal Feature Fusion

结合ARIMA、Prophet、GBDT和LSTM等多种预测模型的优势，融入价格、促销、事件和库存位置等因果特征，构建更精准的长期预测模型，捕捉不同时间尺度的模式和趋势。 Combine the advantages of multiple forecasting models such as ARIMA, Prophet, GBDT, and LSTM, integrate causal features including price, promotion, events, and inventory location, build a more accurate long-term forecasting model, and capture patterns and trends across different time scales.

  • 提升长期预测准确性 Improve long-term forecasting accuracy
  • 更好地捕捉季节性和趋势变化 Better capture seasonal and trend changes
  • 增强对外部因素的响应能力 Enhance responsiveness to external factors

分层一致性重整 Hierarchical Consistency Reconciliation

采用Top-down、Bottom-up和MinT等多种方法进行预测的分层一致性重整，确保不同层级（如产品、区域、渠道）的预测结果协调一致，支持各级决策的有效衔接。 Adopt multiple methods such as Top-down, Bottom-up, and MinT for hierarchical consistency reconciliation of forecasts, ensuring coordinated and consistent forecast results across different levels (e.g., product, region, channel) to support effective connection of decision-making at all levels.

  • 提高预测的整体一致性 Improve overall forecast consistency
  • 增强计划的可行性和可执行性 Enhance plan feasibility and executability
  • 便于不同层级间的协同决策 Facilitate collaborative decision-making across levels

短期Sensing与动态校正 Short-Term Sensing & Dynamic Calibration

通过高频信号监测进行短期Sensing，实时捕捉市场变化，并据此对长期预测进行动态校正，使长期规划能够及时响应市场波动，保持灵活性和适应性。 Conduct short-term sensing through high-frequency signal monitoring to capture market changes in real time, and dynamically calibrate long-term forecasts based on this, enabling long-term planning to respond to market fluctuations in a timely manner and maintain flexibility and adaptability.

  • 提高长期预测的动态适应性 Improve dynamic adaptability of long-term forecasts
  • 减少预测偏差累积效应 Reduce cumulative effect of forecast deviations
  • 增强对市场突变的响应能力 Enhance responsiveness to sudden market changes

FVA评估与闭环优化 FVA Evaluation & Closed-Loop Optimization

实施预测价值添加(FVA)评估，结合门禁规则，持续监控预测质量并进行闭环回灌，不断优化预测模型和流程，实现预测能力的持续提升。 Implement Forecast Value Added (FVA) evaluation, combined with gatekeeping rules, continuously monitor forecast quality and conduct closed-loop feedback, continuously optimize forecast models and processes, and achieve continuous improvement of forecasting capabilities.

  • 量化预测对业务的实际价值 Quantify the actual business value of forecasts
  • 建立持续改进的预测机制 Establish a continuous improvement forecasting mechanism
  • 确保预测模型的稳健性和可靠性 Ensure the robustness and reliability of forecast models

关键行业 KPI 与衡量 Key Industry KPIs & Measurement

长期预测方案重点关注的KPI包括：平均绝对百分比误差(MAPE)、加权绝对百分比误差(WAPE)、预测偏差(Bias)、预测价值添加(FVA)、服务水平、承诺稳定性和计划稳定性等。 The KPIs focused on by long-term forecasting solutions include: Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE), Weighted Absolute Percentage Error (WAPE), Forecast Bias, Forecast Value Added (FVA), Service Level, Commitment Stability, and Planning Stability.

这些KPI可分为三类：1）预测准确性指标（MAPE、WAPE、Bias）；2）预测价值指标（FVA、服务水平提升）；3）运营稳定性指标（承诺稳定性、计划稳定性）。 These KPIs can be divided into three categories: 1) Forecast Accuracy Indicators (MAPE, WAPE, Bias); 2) Forecast Value Indicators (FVA, Service Level Improvement); 3) Operational Stability Indicators (Commitment Stability, Planning Stability).

实际行业案例 Practical Industry Cases

沃尔玛：机器学习驱动的长期库存规划与需求预测 Walmart: Machine Learning-Driven Long-Term Inventory Planning and Demand Forecasting

沃尔玛部署了基于机器学习的长期预测系统，整合了多种预测模型和外部数据信号，显著提升了其库存规划的准确性。该系统能够处理数万种商品的长期需求预测，考虑季节性、促销活动、地区差异和宏观经济因素等多重变量。 Walmart deployed a machine learning-based long-term forecasting system that integrates multiple forecasting models and external data signals, significantly improving the accuracy of its inventory planning. The system can handle long-term demand forecasting for tens of thousands of products, considering multiple variables such as seasonality, promotional activities, regional differences, and macroeconomic factors.

实施后，沃尔玛的库存周转率提升了10-15%，同时缺货率下降了约8%。系统的分层预测能力使不同层级的决策者能够获得一致且相关的预测信息，支持更协调的采购和库存策略。 After implementation, Walmart's inventory turnover rate increased by 10-15%, while the out-of-stock rate decreased by approximately 8%. The system's hierarchical forecasting capability enables decision-makers at different levels to obtain consistent and relevant forecasting information, supporting more coordinated procurement and inventory strategies.
