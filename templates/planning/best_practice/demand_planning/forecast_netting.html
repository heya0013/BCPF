AI Application Scenarios

智能预测净值计算

Intelligent Forecast Netting Calculation

结合多模型集成预测结果，自动进行预测净值计算，考虑现有库存、在途库存、已承诺订单和预期退货等因素，生成准确的净需求预测，为采购和生产计划提供可靠依据。

Integrating multi-model ensemble forecast results, it automatically calculates forecast netting, considering factors such as existing inventory, in-transit inventory, committed orders, and expected returns to generate accurate net demand forecasts, providing a reliable basis for procurement and production planning.

  • 提高净需求预测准确性 | Enhance net demand forecast accuracy
  • 减少人工计算错误和延迟 | Reduce manual calculation errors and delays
  • 确保供需平衡分析的精准性 | Ensure accuracy of supply-demand balance analysis

Cannibalization模型与调整

Cannibalization Model & Adjustment

应用产品替代(Cannibalization)模型，识别和量化产品之间的替代效应，自动调整相关产品的预测净值，避免过度订购或库存积压，特别适用于存在替代关系的产品组合。

Applying the product cannibalization model to identify and quantify substitution effects between products, it automatically adjusts the forecast netting of related products to avoid over-ordering or inventory backlogs, especially suitable for product portfolios with substitution relationships.

  • 优化产品组合的整体库存水平 | Optimize overall inventory levels of product portfolios
  • 减少因产品替代导致的预测偏差 | Reduce forecast deviations caused by product substitution
  • 提高交叉销售和替代销售的响应能力 | Enhance responsiveness to cross-selling and substitution sales

情景仿真与预测调整

Scenario Simulation & Forecast Adjustment

通过情景仿真技术模拟不同市场条件、供应链中断和政策变化对预测净值的影响，生成多种可能的净值预测方案，支持管理层进行风险评估和决策制定。

Using scenario simulation technology to simulate the impact of different market conditions, supply chain disruptions, and policy changes on forecast netting, it generates multiple possible netting forecast scenarios to support management in risk assessment and decision-making.

  • 增强供应链的抗风险能力 | Enhance the risk resistance of the supply chain
  • 提高预测对不确定性的适应性 | Improve forecast adaptability to uncertainties
  • 支持主动式风险管理和应对 | Support proactive risk management and response

智能校验与异常处理

Intelligent Verification & Exception Handling

实施FVA评估和门禁规则，对预测净值进行自动校验，识别异常值并触发相应处理流程，通过闭环回灌机制持续改进预测净值计算模型，提高长期可靠性。

Implementing Forecast Value Added (FVA) evaluation and gatekeeping rules to automatically verify forecast netting, identify outliers, and trigger corresponding processing workflows. Through a closed-loop feedback mechanism, it continuously improves the forecast netting calculation model to enhance long-term reliability.

  • 提高预测净值的可信度 | Enhance the credibility of forecast netting
  • 缩短异常处理和解决周期 | Shorten exception handling and resolution cycles
  • 建立持续改进的预测机制 | Establish a continuously improving forecasting mechanism

关键行业 KPI 与衡量

Key Industry KPIs & Measurement

预测净值方案重点关注的KPI包括：预测价值添加(FVA)、承诺稳定性、改期率、缺货率、计划稳定性、异常关闭周期、服务水平和库存准确性等。

The key KPIs focused on by the forecast netting solution include: Forecast Value Added (FVA), commitment stability, rescheduling rate, stockout rate, plan stability, exception closure cycle, service level, and inventory accuracy.

这些KPI可分为三类：1）预测质量指标（FVA、预测准确性）；2）运营效率指标（改期率、异常关闭周期）；3）服务与稳定性指标（承诺稳定性、计划稳定性、服务水平）。

These KPIs can be categorized into three types: 1) Forecast quality indicators (FVA, forecast accuracy); 2) Operational efficiency indicators (rescheduling rate, exception closure cycle); 3) Service and stability indicators (commitment stability, plan stability, service level).

实际行业案例

Actual Industry Cases

西门子：智能预测净值系统在工业供应链中的应用

Siemens: Application of Intelligent Forecast Netting System in Industrial Supply Chain

西门子在其工业产品供应链中部署了AI驱动的预测净值系统，整合了来自多个渠道的需求信号、库存数据和供应链状态信息，自动计算精确的净需求预测。系统特别关注了产品替代效应和长周期采购项目的预测调整。

Siemens deployed an AI-driven forecast netting system in its industrial product supply chain, integrating demand signals, inventory data, and supply chain status information from multiple channels to automatically calculate accurate net demand forecasts. The system specifically focused on product substitution effects and forecast adjustments for long-cycle procurement items.

实施后，西门子的预测净值准确性提升了25%，订单改期率下降了18%，库存周转率提高了15%。系统的情景仿真功能使供应链团队能够更好地应对市场波动和供应中断，异常情况的解决周期缩短了30%以上。

After implementation, Siemens achieved a 25% improvement in forecast netting accuracy, an 18% reduction in order rescheduling rates, and a 15% increase in inventory turnover. The system's scenario simulation capabilities enabled the supply chain team to better respond to market fluctuations and supply disruptions, reducing the resolution cycle for abnormal situations by over 30%.
