AI 应用场景 AI Application Scenarios

规则 + 约束优化 + ML 融合的 Allocation 引擎 Rule + Constraint Optimization + ML Integrated Allocation Engine

将业务规则与实时约束（库存、交期、优先级、运输能力）与机器学习的需求/短缺预测结合，自动给出最优分配（谁先发、从哪个 DC、是否拆单或部分履约），同时保持业务策略可配置。 Combine business rules with real-time constraints (inventory, delivery dates, priorities, transportation capacity) and machine learning demand/shortage predictions to automatically provide optimal allocation (who to ship first, from which DC, whether to split orders or partial fulfillment), while maintaining configurable business strategies.

  • 首次承诺命中率（First Promise Accuracy）提升 First Promise Accuracy Improvement
  • 优先客户履约率提升（High-priority fulfillment） High-Priority Fulfillment Rate Improvement
  • 降低改期率 / 减少加急成本 Reduce Rescheduling Rate / Expedited Costs

情景仿真（缺料 / 突发需求 / 策略切换） Scenario Simulation (Material Shortage / Sudden Demand / Strategy Switching)

在数字孪生或沙箱中模拟多种突发情景（关键零件短缺、促销爆单、运输中断），评估 Allocation 策略的鲁棒性并选择风险最小化的分配方案，支持"一键对比"候选方案。 Simulate various emergency scenarios (critical component shortages, promotional order surges, transportation disruptions) in digital twins or sandboxes, evaluate the robustness of allocation strategies and select risk-minimizing allocation solutions, supporting "one-click comparison" of candidate solutions.

  • 策略变更前后 OTIF 与成本对比 OTIF and Cost Comparison Before and After Strategy Changes
  • 异常订单与加急单占比变化 Changes in Abnormal Orders and Expedited Order Ratios

主数据 + 策略参数管理 Master Data + Strategy Parameter Management

统一管理 SKU 主数据、优先级规则、履约等级与 SLA 约定，支持按客户/渠道/产品线维度自定义策略，并与 Allocation 引擎实时联动。 Unified management of SKU master data, priority rules, fulfillment levels and SLA agreements, supporting customized strategies by customer/channel/product line dimensions, and real-time linkage with the Allocation engine.

  • 承诺一致性与可追溯性 Commitment Consistency and Traceability
  • 策略版本化与实验回滚能力 Strategy Versioning and Experiment Rollback Capability

可解释 AI（XAI）+ 可视化看板 Explainable AI (XAI) + Visual Dashboard

为关键分配决策提供可解释性的原因说明（XAI），在控制塔仪表板展示约束命中、替代方案及预计成本变化，帮助业务快速接受并调优模型。 Provide explainable reasoning (XAI) for key allocation decisions, display constraint hits, alternative solutions, and estimated cost changes on the control tower dashboard, helping business quickly accept and optimize models.

  • XAI：解释为什么把库存分配给 A 客户而非 B 客户 XAI: Explain why inventory is allocated to Customer A instead of Customer B
  • 可视化策略实验结果与回归监控 Visualize Strategy Experiment Results and Regression Monitoring

门限化触发 + 策略实验 Threshold Triggering + Strategy Experiments

通过阈值触发（如库存低于 X、优先级高于 Y）自动切换守护策略；并对候选策略做 A/B 风险实验以量化收益与副作用。 Automatically switch protection strategies through threshold triggering (e.g., inventory below X, priority above Y); and conduct A/B risk experiments on candidate strategies to quantify benefits and side effects.

  • 减少手工干预、缩短异常关闭周期 Reduce Manual Intervention, Shorten Exception Closure Cycle
  • 支持回滚与逐步放量 Support Rollback and Gradual Scaling

关键行业 KPI 与衡量 Key Industry KPIs and Measurement

本方案重点关注的 KPI 包括：首次承诺命中率（First Promise Accuracy）、改期率（Reschedule Rate）、优先客户履约率（High-priority fulfillment）、OTIF、POF（Perfect Order）、加急成本、客户投诉率与争议关闭周期、NPS 以及计划稳定性与波动收敛度。 The key KPIs focused on by this solution include: First Promise Accuracy, Reschedule Rate, High-priority fulfillment, OTIF, POF (Perfect Order), expedited costs, customer complaint rate and dispute closure cycle, NPS, and planning stability and fluctuation convergence.

这些 KPI 可分为三类：1）承诺与履约质量（首次承诺命中率、OTIF、POF、优先客户履约）；2）运营成本与应急（加急成本、异常订单率）；3）客户感受与流程收敛（投诉率、争议关闭周期、NPS、计划稳定性）。 These KPIs can be divided into three categories: 1) Commitment and fulfillment quality (First Promise Accuracy, OTIF, POF, high-priority fulfillment); 2) Operational costs and emergency response (expedited costs, abnormal order rate); 3) Customer experience and process convergence (complaint rate, dispute closure cycle, NPS, planning stability).

实际行业案例（示例） Practical Industry Cases (Examples)

Inditex / Zara：基于实时可见性与数据驱动的分配与补货能力 Inditex / Zara: Real-Time Visibility and Data-Driven Allocation and Replenishment Capabilities

Zara（Inditex）通过在供应链大量部署 RFID 与结合门店/仓库实时数据，显著提升了库存可见性与补货速度，从而为更精准的分配与订单承诺打下基础。RFID 带来的高精度库存使其能够更快识别门店缺货、优先补货与执行更精细的分配策略，间接提升店铺可售率与履约表现。 Zara (Inditex) significantly improved inventory visibility and replenishment speed by extensively deploying RFID in the supply chain and combining store/warehouse real-time data, thereby laying the foundation for more accurate allocation and order commitment. The high-precision inventory brought by RFID enables faster identification of store stockouts, priority replenishment, and execution of more refined allocation strategies, indirectly improving store sell-through rates and fulfillment performance.

公开报道与行业研究显示，RFID 与数据驱动的库存可见性能显著提高库存准确性与可用率，从而支持更高效的 allocation / order-promising 决策（参考链接见下）。该案例说明：要想把 Allocation 引擎落地并实现 OTIF / 首次承诺提升，**先提升主数据和库存可见性（item-level）是关键**。 Public reports and industry research show that RFID and data-driven inventory visibility can significantly improve inventory accuracy and availability, thereby supporting more efficient allocation/order-promising decisions (see reference links below). This case illustrates that to implement the Allocation engine and achieve OTIF/first promise improvements, **first improving master data and item-level inventory visibility is key**.
