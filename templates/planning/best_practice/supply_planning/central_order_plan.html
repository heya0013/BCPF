本页展示中央订单计划模块如何通过 AI 优化订单/分配、提升履约率与效率。 This page demonstrates how the Central Order Plan module optimizes orders/allocation through AI, improving fulfillment rates and efficiency.

AI 应用场景 AI Application Scenarios

规则 + ML 结合的订单分配优化 Rule + ML Integrated Order Allocation Optimization

在中央计划层，AI 根据规则与 ML 模型对订单进行分配优化（如优先客户、物流成本、库存位置）。 At the central planning level, AI optimizes order allocation based on rules and ML models (such as priority customers, logistics costs, inventory locations).

异常检测 + 根因解释 + 行动推荐 Anomaly Detection + Root Cause Explanation + Action Recommendation

当订单执行中出现偏差、供应异常或配送中断时，系统触发根因分析并推荐纠偏策略。 When deviations occur in order execution, supply anomalies, or delivery disruptions, the system triggers root cause analysis and recommends corrective strategies.

约束求解 + 情景规划 Constraint Solving + Scenario Planning

在中央计划中使用情景规划模拟不同需求与供应压力下的最优方案路径。 Use scenario planning in central planning to simulate optimal solution paths under different demand and supply pressures.

Demand Sensing + 供给协同 加持 Demand Sensing + Supply Collaboration Enhancement

引入 Demand Sensing 模型增强订单计划输入，与供应计划协同作用提高整体精度。 Introduce Demand Sensing models to enhance order plan inputs, working synergistically with supply planning to improve overall accuracy.

效果分析（机制视角） Effect Analysis (Mechanism Perspective)

规则 + ML 分配使订单分配更智能；异常检测 + 推荐能够快速纠偏；情景规划提高对突发波动的弹性；Demand Sensing 输入增强进一步降低预测误差，从而提升执行端的稳定性与履约率。 Rule + ML allocation makes order distribution smarter; anomaly detection + recommendation enables quick correction; scenario planning improves resilience to sudden fluctuations; Demand Sensing input enhancement further reduces prediction errors, thereby improving execution stability and fulfillment rates.

McKinsey AI 供应链早期采用者 McKinsey AI Supply Chain Early Adopters

McKinsey 的研究指出：早期采用 AI 的供应链可实现库存下降 35%、服务水平提升 65%、物流成本下降 15%。这些整体改善正是多个模块（预测 / 分配 / 异常响应）共同作用的结果。 McKinsey's research indicates: early AI adopters in supply chains can achieve 35% inventory reduction, 65% service level improvement, and 15% logistics cost reduction. These overall improvements result from the combined effects of multiple modules (forecasting / allocation / anomaly response).
