AI 应用场景 AI Application Scenarios

约束求解 + 情景规划（多目标） Constraint Solving + Scenario Planning (Multi-Objective)

在 CTB 过程中，以利润、服务等级、现金占用为目标进行情景规划与约束求解，自动分配产能优先顺序。 In the CTB process, conduct scenario planning and constraint solving with profit, service level, and cash occupancy as objectives, automatically allocating production capacity priorities.

CTB 智能齐套与替代推荐 CTB Intelligent Kitting and Alternative Recommendations

当某件料缺失或不齐套时，AI 提供替代件推荐或调整方案，确保产能不中断。 When materials are missing or incomplete, AI provides alternative component recommendations or adjustment solutions to ensure uninterrupted production capacity.

异常检测 + 根因解释 + 行动推荐 Anomaly Detection + Root Cause Explanation + Action Recommendation

对齐套失败、物料异常、供应中断等触发 root-cause 分析，并自动建议纠正动作。 Trigger root-cause analysis for kitting failures, material anomalies, supply disruptions, etc., and automatically recommend corrective actions.

规则 + ML 结合的优先分配优化 Rule + ML Integrated Priority Allocation Optimization

在 CTB 产能分配时，融合规则引擎与 ML 模型进行最优分配决策。 During CTB capacity allocation, integrate rule engines with ML models for optimal allocation decisions.

效果分析（机制视角） Effect Analysis (Mechanism Perspective)

通过情景规划 + 约束求解，系统能在不同目标间做折中（利润 vs 服务 vs 现金占用），减少盲目变更；齐套推荐减少因部件不齐造成的产能浪费；异常检测 + 根因分析及时补救，提高 CTB 准确性与稳定性；规则 + ML 分配确保在复杂约束下找到最优产能使用方案。 Through scenario planning + constraint solving, the system can make trade-offs between different objectives (profit vs. service vs. cash occupancy), reducing blind changes; kitting recommendations reduce capacity waste due to incomplete components; anomaly detection + root cause analysis enables timely remediation, improving CTB accuracy and stability; rule + ML allocation ensures optimal capacity utilization solutions under complex constraints.
