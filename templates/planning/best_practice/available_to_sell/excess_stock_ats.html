Excess Stock ATS（过剩 / 滞销库存） Excess Stock ATS (Excess / Slow-Moving Inventory)

识别滞销库存、剔除 ATS 口径、智能处置策略 Identify Slow-Moving Inventory, Exclude from ATS Caliber, Intelligent Disposal Strategy

本页展示在 Excess Stock 场景下，如何利用 AI 提高 ATS 精度、优化库存结构与回收效率。 This page demonstrates how to use AI to improve ATS accuracy, optimize inventory structure, and enhance recovery efficiency in the Excess Stock scenario.

AI 应用场景 AI Application Scenarios

库存健康诊断 + Excess 标识 Inventory Health Diagnosis + Excess Identification

使用机器学习模型识别滞销、超周期或需求下滑 SKU，把这些 SKU 从短期 ATS 口径中剔除或降优先级。 Use machine learning models to identify slow-moving, over-cycle, or demand-declining SKUs, and exclude these SKUs from the short-term ATS caliber or lower their priority.

动态定价 + 促销建议 + 再分配 Dynamic Pricing + Promotion Suggestions + Reallocation

结合价格弹性预测、渠道容量与库存分布，AI 给出最佳促销时机、折扣策略和跨仓调拨建议。 Combining price elasticity prediction, channel capacity, and inventory distribution, AI provides optimal promotion timing, discount strategies, and cross-warehouse transfer suggestions.

异常事件驱动触发 Abnormal Event-Driven Trigger

当促销失利、退货激增或补货滞后发生时，系统自动触发处置机制（促销、回库、批发出清）。 When promotion failures, surge in returns, or delayed replenishment occur, the system automatically triggers disposal mechanisms (promotion, warehouse return, wholesale clearance).

可解释仪表板 + 行动建议 Interpretable Dashboard + Action Suggestions

用 XAI 展示为何某 SKU 被视为 Excess，并自动推行动作建议（如调价/移库/清仓），业务可审查采纳。 Use XAI to show why a certain SKU is regarded as Excess, and automatically push action suggestions (such as price adjustment/warehouse transfer/clearance), which the business can review and adopt.

效果分析（对应业务机制） Effect Analysis (Corresponding Business Mechanism)

通过把滞销 SKU 从 ATS 口径剔除，可以避免这些库存占用承诺能力，从而释放可售空间给高潜 SKU。动态定价/促销 + 再分配组合能加速库存周转、减少损耗与折价成本。 By excluding slow-moving SKUs from the ATS caliber, the commitment capacity occupied by these inventories can be avoided, thereby freeing up sellable space for high-potential SKUs. The combination of dynamic pricing/promotion and reallocation can accelerate inventory turnover and reduce loss and discount costs. 在异常事件驱动下及时响应，能防止库存恶化和资金冻结。可解释与建议机制提高运营采纳率与信任度。 Timely response driven by abnormal events can prevent inventory deterioration and capital freezing. The interpretable and suggestion mechanism improves operational adoption rate and trust.
