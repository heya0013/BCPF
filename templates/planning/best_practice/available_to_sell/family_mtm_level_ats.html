本页展示如何在 Family / 系列 / 月度层面应用 AI 优化 ATS 口径与稳定性。 This page demonstrates how to apply AI to optimize ATS caliber and stability at the Family/Series/Monthly level.

AI 应用场景 AI Application Scenarios

Family / MTM 可售口径统一 Unified Family/MTM Sellable Caliber

按 Family 层级整合所有子 SKU 的可售库存口径，支持跨 SKU 缓冲协调与策略移量。 Integrate the sellable inventory caliber of all sub-SKUs at the Family level to support cross-SKU buffer coordination and strategic volume transfer.

情景仿真 + 策略实验 Scenario Simulation + Strategy Experiment

在 Family 级别做促销 / 供应中断 / 产品切换情景仿真，评估对 OTIF / 波动的影响。 Conduct scenario simulations of promotion/supply disruption/product switching at the Family level to evaluate the impact on OTIF/volatility.

可解释仪表板辅助 S&OP / IBP Interpretable Dashboard Assists S&OP / IBP

将 ATS 预测、波动指标、XAI 解释输出可视化于 S&OP 仪表板，辅助高层做稳定化决策。 Visualize ATS predictions, volatility indicators, and XAI interpretation outputs on the S&OP dashboard to assist senior management in making stabilization decisions.

门限触发 + 决策回滚机制 Threshold Trigger + Decision Rollback Mechanism

设置波动、库存变动等触发阈值，自动触发策略调整或回滚保护机制。 Set trigger thresholds for volatility, inventory changes, etc., to automatically trigger strategy adjustments or rollback protection mechanisms.

效果分析（业务驱动视角） Effect Analysis (Business-Driven Perspective)

在系列层面整合 ATS 口径可减少因单个 SKU 波动造成的系列误差；情景仿真与策略实验能提前规避极端情形；XAI 仪表板帮助高层理解承诺变化原因，减少盲目调整。通过门限触发机制，可降低频繁策略变更带来的波动性损耗。 Integrating ATS caliber at the series level can reduce series errors caused by the volatility of individual SKUs; scenario simulation and strategy experiments can avoid extreme situations in advance; the XAI dashboard helps senior management understand the reasons for commitment changes and reduce blind adjustments. Through the threshold trigger mechanism, the volatility loss caused by frequent strategy changes can be reduced.

某消费电子集团：系列层面稳定化承诺 A Consumer Electronics Group: Series-Level Stabilized Commitment
