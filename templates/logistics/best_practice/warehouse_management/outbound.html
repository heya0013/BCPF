AI Application Effect Analysis

一家全球零售巨头实施了AI驱动的出库货物发放与拣取策略优化系统，通过多目标优化+强化学习/规则优化了承运商选择和运输策略，使用拣选路径优化+装载优化+码头排程减少了等待时间，提高了出库效率。 A global retail giant implemented an AI-driven outbound goods issue and pick up strategy optimization system, using multi-objective optimization + reinforcement learning/rules to optimize carrier selection and transportation strategies, and picking path optimization + loading optimization + dock scheduling to reduce waiting time and improve outbound efficiency.

项目实施后，该企业实现了运输成本降低9%，准点率提升7%，等待时间减少25%，出库效率提升18%，大幅提高了供应链效率和客户满意度。 After project implementation, the company achieved a 9% reduction in transportation costs, a 7% improvement in on-time rate, a 25% reduction in waiting time, and an 18% improvement in outbound efficiency, significantly improving supply chain efficiency and customer satisfaction.
