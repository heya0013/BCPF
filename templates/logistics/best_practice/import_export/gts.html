Import & Export / (GTS) Flow AI Case Study

AI驱动的进出口和全球贸易服务流程优化解决方案，通过网络流、时序/事件流、MIP/CP排程和事件对账技术提高贸易效率，降低运营成本，并增强合规性 AI-driven import and export/global trade services flow optimization solutions improve trade efficiency, reduce operating costs, and enhance compliance through network flow, time series/event flow, MIP/CP scheduling, and event reconciliation technology

AI应用场景
AI Application Scenarios

网络流 + 装载优化 + 情景仿真
Network Flow + Loading Optimization + Scenario Simulation

通过网络流算法优化进出口货物流动路径，使用装载优化技术提高运输效率，结合情景仿真预测各种条件下的表现，降低运输成本，提高载重利用率。 Optimize import and export cargo flow paths through network flow algorithms, use loading optimization technology to improve transportation efficiency, combine scenario simulation to predict performance under various conditions, reduce transportation costs, and improve load utilization.

时序/事件流 + 异常检测 + 规则/优化
Time Series/Event Flow + Anomaly Detection + Rules/Optimization

通过时序和事件流分析监控贸易流程，使用异常检测技术识别潜在问题，结合规则引擎和优化算法提供智能建议和决策支持，提高提前干预率。 Monitor trade processes through time series and event flow analysis, use anomaly detection technology to identify potential problems, combine rule engines and optimization algorithms to provide intelligent suggestions and decision support, and improve early intervention rates.

MIP/CP 排程 + 装载优化
MIP/CP Scheduling + Loading Optimization

通过混合整数规划（MIP）和约束规划（CP）算法优化码头和仓库排程，使用装载优化技术提高资源利用率，减少Dock等待时间，提高吞吐量和劳动效率。 Optimize dock and warehouse scheduling through mixed integer programming (MIP) and constraint programming (CP) algorithms, use loading optimization technology to improve resource utilization, reduce dock waiting time, and improve throughput and labor efficiency.

事件对账 + LLM归因 + RPA
Event Reconciliation + LLM Attribution + RPA

通过事件对账技术确保贸易数据的一致性，使用大语言模型（LLM）进行差异归因分析，结合机器人流程自动化（RPA）自动化对账流程，缩短对账周期，提高追回率。 Ensure the consistency of trade data through event reconciliation technology, use large language models (LLM) for difference attribution analysis, combine robotic process automation (RPA) to automate reconciliation processes, shorten reconciliation cycles, and improve recovery rates.

AI应用场景效果分析
AI Application Effect Analysis

通过AI驱动的进出口和全球贸易服务流程优化解决方案，企业能够显著提高贸易效率，降低运营成本，并增强合规性。网络流算法优化了货物流动路径，装载优化技术提高了运输效率，情景仿真则预测了各种条件下的表现，帮助企业更好地应对不确定性。 Through AI-driven import and export/global trade services flow optimization solutions, enterprises can significantly improve trade efficiency, reduce operating costs, and enhance compliance. Network flow algorithms optimize cargo flow paths, loading optimization technology improves transportation efficiency, and scenario simulation predicts performance under various conditions, helping enterprises better cope with uncertainty.

时序和事件流分析监控了贸易流程，异常检测技术识别了潜在问题，规则引擎和优化算法则提供了智能建议和决策支持，提高了提前干预率和异常处理效率。混合整数规划和约束规划算法优化了码头和仓库排程，减少了Dock等待时间，提高了吞吐量和劳动效率。事件对账技术确保了贸易数据的一致性，大语言模型进行了差异归因分析，机器人流程自动化则自动化了对账流程，缩短了对账周期，提高了追回率。 Time series and event flow analysis monitor trade processes, anomaly detection technology identifies potential problems, rule engines and optimization algorithms provide intelligent suggestions and decision support, improving early intervention rates and anomaly handling efficiency. Mixed integer programming and constraint programming algorithms optimize dock and warehouse scheduling, reduce dock waiting time, and improve throughput and labor efficiency. Event reconciliation technology ensures the consistency of trade data, large language models perform difference attribution analysis, and robotic process automation automates reconciliation processes, shortening reconciliation cycles and improving recovery rates.
