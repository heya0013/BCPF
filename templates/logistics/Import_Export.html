核心价值 Core Value

运输计划 / 承运商选择 / 费率管理决定时效与成本，是端到端履约的主干流程。；关务合规与清关能力是国际运输的门禁，决定是否按期入境 / 出境。 Transportation planning, carrier selection, and rate management determine timeliness and costs, and serve as the core process of end-to-end fulfillment. ; Customs compliance and customs clearance capabilities are the gateway to international transportation, determining whether entry/exit can be completed on schedule.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 在现有网络与费率下，最优化多站中转、拼箱/合单与波次，平衡时效与成本。 Under the existing network and rate framework, optimize multi-stop transshipment, LCL (Less than Container Load)/order consolidation, and wave planning to balance timeliness and costs
  • 基于轨迹 / 口岸 / 天气预测延误，自动触发改配或加急 Predict delays based on tracking data, port events, and weather conditions, and automatically trigger re-routing or expedited handling.
  • 将波次、拣选、装车、码头预约一体化排程 Conduct integrated scheduling of wave planning, picking, loading, and terminal appointments.
  • 按里程碑 / 轨迹对承运商账单自动对账；异常自动发起索赔 Automatically reconcile carrier bills based on milestones/tracking data; automatically initiate claims for abnormalities.
