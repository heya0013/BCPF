物流采购到付款 In-bound to MFG

覆盖从采购需求到向供应商付款全流程的物流与财务协同管理，确保采供环节高效闭环 Cover the collaborative management of logistics and finance throughout the entire process from procurement demand to payment to suppliers, ensuring an efficient closed loop in the procurement and supply process.

物流合同管理 Logistics Contract Management
核心价值 Core Value

费率 / 附加费 / 合同是审计基线，决定计费合规与真实成本。；应付 / 付款 / 过账承接审计结果，形成财务闭环。 Rates/surcharges/contracts serve as the audit baseline, determining billing compliance and actual costs; Accounts payable/payment/posting follow the audit results to form a financial closed-loop.

运费审计、支付与索赔 Freight Audit & Payment & Claim
核心价值 Core Value

账单 / 发票是对账对象，需与里程碑与合同条款一致。；审计 / 勾稽（3-Way Match）控制差错与超付，是 P2P 核心控制点。；争议 / 差异管理是追回与扣减机制，降低异常损失。 Bills/invoices are the objects of reconciliation and must be consistent with milestones and contract terms; Auditing/three-way match controls errors and overpayments, and is a core control point of P2P; Dispute/discrepancy management is a recovery and deduction mechanism to reduce abnormal losses.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 结合承运商信用、历史争议、金额阈值与异常置信度，动态调整审批路由与自动放款阈值 Dynamically adjust approval routes and automatic payment release thresholds by integrating carrier credit, historical disputes, amount thresholds, and anomaly confidence
  • 将承运商账单与 TMS 里程碑 / POD、GTC 税费 / 放行记录自动勾稽，实时标注差异并生成归因与处置建议 Automatically reconcile carrier bills with TMS milestones/PODs and GTC tax/clearance records, mark discrepancies in real time, and generate attribution and disposal suggestions.
  • 基于历史争议 / 索赔标签训练分类模型，联动扣减、补差、复议与承运商绩效 Train classification models based on historical dispute/claim tags, and link deductions, supplements, reviews, and carrier performance.
