全球贸易合规 Global_Trade_Compliance

确保跨国贸易行为符合各国进出口法律、关税政策及贸易管制规定，防范合规风险。 Ensure that cross-border trade activities comply with the import and export laws, tariff policies, and trade control regulations of various countries to prevent compliance risks.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

许可证管控 License Control
核心价值 Core Value

单证 / 要素完整性与一致性（发票 / 装箱单 / 提单 / COO/Incoterms）是归类与申报的前置门禁。；许可 / 批文（监管条件、双抬头等）不合规将导致口岸拦截与延误。；运费审计将放行 / 税费与账单、回单 / POD 比对，关闭财务风险。 The completeness and consistency of documents/elements (invoice/packing list/bill of lading/COO/Incoterms) are the precondition for classification and declaration; Non-compliance of permits/approvals (supervision requirements, dual-entity declaration, etc.) will lead to port detention and delays; Freight auditing compares customs release/duties with bills, delivery receipts/POD to eliminate financial risks.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 融合口岸 / 港口事件与在途轨迹，提前发现查验 / 拥堵，自动调整舱单 / 申报与改配 Integrate port/harbor events and in-transit tracking data to identify inspections/congestion in advance, and automatically adjust manifests/declarations and re-routing.
