仓库管理 Warehouse Management

管理仓库内货物的存储、收发、库存盘点及库位规划等，确保仓储作业的精准与高效。 Manage the storage, receiving and dispatching, inventory counting, and location planning of goods in the warehouse to ensure the accuracy and efficiency of warehouse operations.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

入库货物接收与上架策略 Inbound Goods Receive & Put Away Strategy
核心价值 Core Value

运输计划/承运商选择/费率管理决定时效与成本，是端到端履约的主干流程；仓内入库/拣选/波次/装载与码头排程直接影响出入库效率与发运达成。 Transport planning/carrier selection/rate management determine timeliness and cost, which is the backbone process of end-to-end fulfillment; warehouse inbound/picking/wave/loading and dock scheduling directly affect inbound and outbound efficiency and shipping achievement.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 融合承运商实时轨迹、历史时效、天气与口岸拥堵预测 POD 时间，并对延误提前预警与改配 Integrate carrier real-time tracking, historical timeliness, weather and port congestion predictions for POD time, and provide early warnings and rescheduling for delays.
  • 基于产品主数据自动判定 HS / 原产地并校验禁限清单，降低通关异常 Automatically determine HS code/origin based on product master data and verify prohibited/restricted lists to reduce customs clearance exceptions.
  • 将承运商账单与执行里程碑 / 轨迹自动对账，异常自动发起索赔 Automatically reconcile carrier bills with execution milestones/tracking, and automatically initiate claims for exceptions.

出库货物发放与拣取策略 Outbound Goods Issue & Pick Up Strategy
核心价值 Core Value

运输计划/承运商选择/费率管理决定时效与成本，是端到端履约的主干流程。 Transport planning/carrier selection/rate management determine timeliness and cost, which is the backbone process of end-to-end fulfillment.

优化建议： Optimization Suggestions:
  • 根据价格 - 时效 - 可靠性多目标优化承运商选择与合单 / 拼箱策略 Optimize carrier selection and consolidation/container sharing strategies based on multi-objective optimization of price, timeliness, and reliability.
  • 将 WMS 波次、拣选路径、装车与码头预约一体化求解，减少等待与拥堵 Integrate WMS wave, picking path, loading, and dock appointment scheduling to reduce waiting and congestion.
  • 利用AI预测模型优化库存布局，减少拣货路径长度 Use AI prediction models to optimize inventory layout and reduce picking path length.
