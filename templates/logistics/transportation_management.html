运输管理 Transportation Management

对货物运输的方式、承运商、路线及在途情况进行全面管控，保障运输环节的高效与可靠 Conduct comprehensive management and control over the mode of cargo transportation, carriers, routes, and in-transit status to ensure the efficiency and reliability of the transportation link.

关键流程模块 Key Process Modules

订单管理 Order Management
核心价值 Core Value

运输计划/承运商选择/费率管理决定时效与成本，是端到端履约的主干流程。 Transport planning/carrier selection/rate management determine timeliness and cost, which is the backbone process of end-to-end fulfillment; warehouse inbound/picking/wave/loading and dock scheduling directly affect inbound and outbound efficiency and shipping achievement.
